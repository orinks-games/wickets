# The Echo

## About the game

Down in the parlor's cellar sits the Echo, a musical machine whose pipes have drifted out of tune. Following Theo's notes, you work up the rank from low to high, nudging each pipe until its wobble settles still and locking it in place.

Once every pipe holds its true note, the Echo plays the whole rank back as a tune. You can tune at your own pace, and the Echo opens in act 4 of story mode.

## The wobble

Each pipe sounds alongside the target note it should match, creating a steady wobble while it is out of tune. Raise or lower the pipe until the two notes lock together smoothly. Press Tab whenever you want to check the target note on its own, or open Sound check from the title to compare the tuning cues.

## Tuning a pipe

Press Up to raise the pipe one step, or Down to lower it one step. Each step is 5 cents, a twentieth of a semitone, and every pipe starts a few steps sharp or flat. Press H if you want to check which way to adjust it and how many steps out it is.

Press Space to set the pipe once it matches its target note. If it is still out of tune, keep nudging it until the wobble stops. The game lets you know you are nearly there when you are just one step away.

## The rank

A rank is a row of pipes arranged from low to high. As soon as you set a pipe in tune, you move automatically to the next pipe that still needs work. You can also press Left or Right to step down and up the rank yourself, and press R to check the current pipe and its note.

## Scoring and records

Each pipe set in tune earns 10 points. Trying to set a pipe while it still wobbles costs 2 points, and each hint costs 3 points, though every tuned pipe still scores at least 2 points.

Setting a pipe on your first try with no hint adds to your first-try total. Open Records from the title to review your best score, the ranks and pipes you have tuned, and your challenge results.

## Hints

Press H to check whether the pipe is sharp or flat and how many steps out it sits. In evening tuning and challenges, a hint costs 3 points unless the pipe is already in tune. In the practice rank, hints are free.

## The practice rank

The practice rank gives you 4 pipes with free hints and no score, so you can get comfortable judging the wobble at your own speed.

## Challenges

Every player receives the exact same rank for each period, down to the notes and how far each pipe starts out of tune.

- Tonight's rank, daily: 6 pipes, each 3 to 10 steps out. Changes every day.
- The swell organ, weekly: 8 pipes, each 4 to 14 steps out. Changes every Monday.
- The full Echo, monthly: all 10 pipes, each 1 to 18 steps out. Changes on the first of every month.

Choose Leave the challenge from the pause menu to stop without recording the rank, so you can come back and try again later.

## Controls

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's instructions follow your bindings.

- Up arrow: raise the pipe one step.
- Down arrow: lower the pipe one step.
- Space: set the pipe once the wobble is still.
- Left arrow: move to the next pipe down the rank.
- Right arrow: move to the next pipe up the rank.
- Tab: play the target note alone for a moment.
- S: check the current pipe, whether it wobbles, and how many pipes are in tune. Press S again for the rank and your score.
- R: repeat the current pipe and its note.
- H: check whether the pipe is sharp or flat and by how many steps.
- F1: check what the keys do on the current screen. F2 stops speech.
- Escape: pause for Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, or Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title becomes Leave the challenge.
