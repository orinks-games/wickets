## About the game

The Echo is the listening machine in the parlor's cellar, and its pipes have drifted out of tune. Theo's notes say how to fix them: low to high, and listen for the wobble to go still. Raise or lower each pipe until it holds its note, then set it. Once every pipe in the rank is in tune, the Echo plays them back as a tune. Nothing fails here. The Echo opens in story mode, in act 4.

## The wobble

While you tune a pipe, two steady organ tones play together: the pipe, and the true note it should hold. When the pipe is out of tune, the two tones wobble against each other, a slow pulse in the sound. The farther out the pipe is, the faster the wobble. As the pipe comes near its note the wobble slows, and when it is in tune the wobble stops and you hear one still tone. Tab plays the true note alone for a moment, if you want to hear what the pipe is aiming for.

## Tuning a pipe

Up raises the pipe one step and Down lowers it, with a tiny brass tick. A step is 5 cents, a twentieth of a semitone. Each pipe starts a few steps sharp or flat. If the wobble gets faster, you are going the wrong way. When the wobble is still, Space sets the pipe, and a clear bell says it is in tune. If it still wobbles, a dull thud says not yet, and one step away the game says nearly.

## The rank

A rank is a row of pipes from low to high. When you set a pipe, the game moves you on to the next pipe that needs tuning. Left and Right move down and up the rank by hand, and R names the pipe and its note.

## Scoring and records

Each pipe set in tune is worth 10 points. Each try at setting it while it still wobbles costs 2, and each hint costs 3, but a pipe in tune always scores at least 2. A pipe set at the first try with no hint counts toward your first-try total. Records keeps your best score, the ranks and pipes you have tuned, and your challenge results.

## Hints

H says whether the pipe is sharp or flat and how many steps out it is, with a soft felt knock. In the evening tuning and the challenges a hint costs 3 points, unless the pipe is already in tune. In the practice rank hints are free.

## The practice rank

The practice rank has 4 pipes, free hints and no score. Use it to learn the sound of the wobble.

## Challenges

Every player gets the same rank for each period, notes and offsets included.

Tonight's rank, the daily: 6 pipes, each 3 to 10 steps out. A new one comes every day.

The swell organ, the weekly: 8 pipes, each 4 to 14 steps out. A new one comes every Monday.

The full Echo, the monthly: all 10 pipes, each 1 to 18 steps out. A new one comes on the first of every month.

Leave the challenge, on the pause menu, stops without counting the rank, so you can try again later.

## Controls

Up arrow: raise the pipe one step.

Down arrow: lower the pipe one step.

Space: set the pipe, once the wobble is still.

Left arrow: move to the next pipe down the rank.

Right arrow: move to the next pipe up the rank.

Tab: play the true note alone for a moment.

S: status: the pipe, whether it wobbles, and the pipes in tune. Press it again for the rank and your score.

R: name the pipe and its note.

H: say whether the pipe is sharp or flat, and by how many steps.

F1: help for what you are doing right now. F2 stops speech.

Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title is Leave the challenge.

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's spoken instructions follow your bindings.
