## About the game

The lamps along the Long Hall have gone dark, and Mabel has handed you her old wax taper to bring them back. Walk down the hall, find each unlit lamp in the alcoves on either wall, and reach out to light it before your taper burns down. Even if the taper runs out, Mabel lights you a fresh one so you can always finish the hall. Lamplighter opens in story mode on opening night.

## The hall

The hall stretches step by step from the lobby door to the far wall. At every step there is an alcove on the left wall and one on the right, holding either a lamp or bare wall. You cannot walk past the lobby door or the far wall.

## Finding the lamps

Each unlit lamp calls out every second and a half from its own wall, ahead of you or behind you, growing stronger as you get closer. You can pick up lamps from up to 4 steps away. Lit lamps let you know as you pass them, while bare alcoves stay quiet.

## Lighting a lamp

Left reaches to the left wall and Right reaches to the right wall. If there is an unlit lamp in that alcove, you light it and learn how many are still out. Reaching toward a bare wall or a lamp that is already burning leaves everything as it is.

## The taper

In the evening round and the challenges, your taper burns down while you work. Press Space to light it and start the clock; until then you can take your bearings from the doorway, but you cannot walk down the hall. A warning marks when 20 seconds remain. If the taper burns out, Mabel hands you a fresh one so you can finish the hall, and only the taper bonus is lost. The practice hall has no taper.

## Draughts

Every 4 lamps you light, a draught sweeps through a doorway and blows a lamp out somewhere in the hall. The game tells you where that lamp is from your step. The evening round has 1 draught, and the challenges have 1 to 3.

## Scoring and records

Every lamp you light scores 10 points, including any lamp blown out by a draught. If you light the whole hall on your first taper, every second left on it adds 1 bonus point. Records tracks your best score, your fastest hall, total lamps lit and your challenge results.

## The practice hall

The practice hall is 8 steps long with 4 lamps out, with no taper and no score so you can learn at your own pace.

## Challenges

Every player gets the exact same hall for each challenge period, with the same lamps and draughts.

Tonight's hall, the daily challenge: 16 steps, 8 lamps out, a taper of 1 minute 40 seconds and 1 draught. A new hall arrives every day.

The east wing, the weekly challenge: 24 steps, 12 lamps out, a taper of 2 minutes 30 seconds and 2 draughts. A new hall arrives every Monday.

The grand gallery, the monthly challenge: 32 steps, 16 lamps out, a taper of 3 minutes 30 seconds and 3 draughts. A new hall arrives on the first of every month.

Choosing Leave the challenge from the pause menu stops without counting the run, so you can come back and try again later.

## Controls

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's instructions follow your bindings.

- Up arrow: walk one step toward the far wall.
- Down arrow: walk one step back toward the lobby door.
- Left arrow: reach to the left wall and light the lamp there if it is out.
- Right arrow: reach to the right wall and light the lamp there if it is out.
- Space: light your taper and start the round.
- S: status with lamps still out, your step and taper time left. Press S again for the hall's length and your score.
- R: check what is on the left and right walls at your current step.
- H: point to the nearest unlit lamp.
- F1: context help for what you are doing right now. F2 stops speech.
- Escape: pause for Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title becomes Leave the challenge.
