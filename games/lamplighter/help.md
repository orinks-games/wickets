## About the game

The lamps along the Long Hall have gone out, and Mabel has handed you her old taper. Walk the hall, listen for each lamp that is out, and reach to its wall to light it. The round ends when every lamp is burning. Nothing fails here: if your taper burns down, Mabel lights you a fresh one. Lamplighter opens in story mode, on opening night.

## The hall

The hall is a row of steps from the lobby door to the far wall. At each step there is an alcove on the left wall and one on the right. An alcove holds a lamp, or it is bare wall. You cannot walk beyond the lobby door or the far wall.

## Hearing the lamps

An unlit lamp signals every second and a half or so. You hear it from its own wall, ahead of you or behind you, and louder the nearer you are. You hear lamps up to 4 steps away. A lit lamp has its own cue as you pass it. Bare wall makes no sound.

## Lighting a lamp

Left reaches to the left wall and Right to the right. If there is an unlit lamp there, you light it and hear how many remain. Reaching toward a bare wall or a lit lamp changes nothing.

## The taper

In the evening round and the challenges, the taper burns while you work. Space lights it and starts the clock, and until then you can listen but not move. A warning tells you when the taper has 20 seconds left. If it burns down, Mabel lights you a fresh one. You carry on, and only the taper bonus is lost. The practice hall has no taper.

## Draughts

Now and then a long gust through a doorway blows a lamp out somewhere in the hall, after every 4 lamps you light. The game says where the lamp is from you. The evening round has 1 draught, and the challenges have 1 to 3.

## Scoring and records

Each lamp lit is 10 points, a lamp blown out by a draught included. Light the whole hall on the first taper, and every second left on it is 1 more point. Records keeps your best score, your fastest hall, the lamps you have lit, and your challenge results.

## The practice hall

The practice hall is 8 steps long with 4 lamps out, no taper and no score. Use it to learn the sounds.

## Challenges

Every player gets the same hall for each period, lamps and draughts included.

Tonight's hall, the daily: 16 steps, 8 lamps out, a taper of 1 minute 40 seconds, and 1 draught. A new one comes every day.

The east wing, the weekly: 24 steps, 12 lamps out, a taper of 2 minutes 30 seconds, and 2 draughts. A new one comes every Monday.

The grand gallery, the monthly: 32 steps, 16 lamps out, a taper of 3 minutes 30 seconds, and 3 draughts. A new one comes on the first of every month.

Leave the challenge, on the pause menu, stops without counting the hall, so you can try again later.

## Controls

Up arrow: walk one step toward the far wall.

Down arrow: walk one step back toward the lobby door.

Left arrow: reach to the left wall, and light the lamp there if it is out.

Right arrow: reach to the right wall, and light the lamp there if it is out.

Space: light your taper and start the round.

S: status: the lamps out, your step and the taper time left. Press it again for the hall's length and your score.

R: what is at your step, on the left wall and the right.

H: where the nearest lamp out is, with its whistle from that place.

F1: help for what you are doing right now. F2 stops speech.

Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title is Leave the challenge.

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's spoken instructions follow your bindings.
