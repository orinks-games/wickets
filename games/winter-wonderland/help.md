# Winter Wonderland

## About the game

Winter holidays: sort gifts by sound, then answer children's letters. Gift Sort: clear the shift before the clock runs out. The Wish Desk: find each letter's gift on the shelf and wrap it, with no clock. Midnight Countdown, a rhythm game for New Year's Eve, has its own row on the title.

## The two parts

Winter Wonderland has two parts. Gift Sort happens on the workshop belt, against the clock. The Wish Desk comes after, with no clock: children's letters, and a shelf of gifts with no labels. Story mode plays three Gift Sort shifts and then a stack at the Wish Desk, and scores them together.

Midnight Countdown is a rhythm game for New Year's Eve: the band plays a short rhythm and you answer it on the beat, all the way to midnight. It is not part of the story or the challenges.

## Gift Sort: the belt

Before each shift you hear the briefing: how many gifts to sort, how long the shift clock runs, and who is waiting. Each person stands on your left, straight ahead or on your right, and wishes for one thing. Press Space to start the belt. Each gift rides toward the belt's edge. If it falls off, it breaks and the clock loses 4 seconds.

## Gift Sort: shaking and sending

Space shakes the gift so you can identify it. Gifts include blocks, teddy bears, teacups and bells. Later shifts add sweets, snow globes, clocks, squeaky toys, books and music boxes. Left sends it to the person on your left, Up to the person ahead, and Right to the person on your right. A wrong delivery costs time and leaves the gift on the belt for another try. From shift 3, use Down to return gifts nobody wants. In the first two shifts, the game also names each gift after you shake it.

## Gift Sort: shifts and scoring

There are 8 shifts. Each one has more gifts, more kinds of sound and a faster belt, and the belt speeds up as you sort. The first three briefings say what each wish sounds like. From shift 4 you must work it out. From shift 5, someone changes their mind half way through: their new wish is announced. A delivery scores more the sooner you make it and the longer your streak, times the shift number. Clearing a shift adds a bonus for the time left on the clock. Shifts you reach stay open: choose a starting shift with Left and Right on the Gift Sort row of the title.

## Gift Sort: pace

The belt, each gift's ride and the shift clock hold while the wishes, a change of plans or another line is still being spoken. This holds at every pace and in every Gift Sort run.

Pace in Settings applies to Gift Sort only, and sets how fast it runs: relaxed, normal or quick. Normal is the default and a little slower than quick. Quick keeps the original timings, and relaxed runs at half speed. The Wish Desk has no clock, and Midnight Countdown keeps the band's beat at every pace.

A change applies from your next run. A run keeps the pace it started at through all its shifts, and on to the Wish Desk in the story.

Shifts open at every pace. Relaxed scores are saved separately, including the story and challenges. Normal and quick share their scores.

## The Wish Desk: letters and the shelf

A stack is a pile of letters. Each child asks for one thing, in their own words, and the letter says how old they are. The gifts are on a shelf in front of you, one box in each place. Walk the shelf with the Left and Right arrows, or jump to its ends with Up and Down. Space shakes the box in front of you. In the first two stacks, each letter also says how its wish would sound in a box. From stack 3 you must work it out.

## The Wish Desk: wrapping

When a box sounds like the wish, hold Space to wrap it. Keep holding until it is wrapped. Let go early and nothing is wrapped. The right gift answers the letter, and the next letter comes straight away. A wrong box goes back on the shelf and the letter stays. A wrapped gift leaves an empty place on the shelf.

## The Wish Desk: stacks and scoring

There are 5 stacks, each with more letters and more boxes. Each letter is worth up to 750 points, times the stack number. Every wrong box costs 250 of them, and shakes past about half the shelf cost 50 each. A stack with no wrong box earns a bonus. H names the sound of the wish, for half the letter's points, and asked again it says which way to walk. Answering a stack opens the next: choose a stack with Left and Right on the Wish Desk row of the title.

## Late post

Once each evening, by your computer's clock, a small extra sack of post arrives at the Wish Desk. The first stack you finish from the title in the evening gets two more letters, each worth double. The Wish Desk row's help says when late post is still due tonight.

## Muddled post

On the fourth Wednesday of every month, the post is muddled: the letters come from the youngest children, in very few words, and every box is packed in straw. The first shake of a box is only straw, so shake each one twice. Muddled post has its own row on the title, which reads locked on other days and says when it comes next. It plays stack 3, and keeps its own best.

## Midnight Countdown: call and answer

The countdown starts at ten minutes to midnight. Each minute, the band plays a call one bar long, then you answer it in the next bar: the same instruments on the same beats. The count marks each beat and the start of each bar. Bells are Space. At seven minutes to midnight the drums join, answered with the Down arrow. At four minutes the horns join, answered with the Up arrow. The music stops while each new instrument is introduced, and waits for you to press Space. You can try the drum and horn while it waits. In the final seconds the full orchestra plays one long two-bar call with every instrument, for double points.

## Midnight Countdown: scoring and energy

A note answered right on the beat is perfect. A little early or late is good. Each correct answer adds to your combo, and every 8 in a row raise the score multiplier, up to times 4. Missing a note or playing the wrong instrument breaks the combo and drains party energy. If the energy runs out, the party ends before midnight. Reach midnight to open the next night. There are 5 nights, each faster with trickier rhythms. From the third night, some notes fall between beats.

## Story mode

Story mode plays shifts 1 to 3 of Gift Sort, a short bridge, then stack 2 at the Wish Desk. Your story score is both parts added together. Finish the story once to open the blizzard story: shifts 4 to 6, then stack 4.

## Challenges

Choose Challenges on the title, or Challenges in the parlor's lobby, for three runs that are the same for every player. Each row says what the challenge is, then whether you have played it, your best and your streak. The briefing states the twist and how long until the next one before you play. Your result counts when the run ends, win or lose. Choosing Leave the challenge on the pause menu does not count. Escape on a briefing goes back without starting. Afterwards you hear your score, your best on that challenge and your streak, then the results menu offers the same challenge again or Back to the challenges.

The daily challenge alternates. On even days of the month it is the rush: one Gift Sort shift at shift 5. On odd days it is the morning post: stack 3 at the Wish Desk. Each day brings one twist. On the belt, the twist is one of these. Rush hour: the belt runs a quarter faster. Short clock: four fifths of the usual time. Big order: three extra gifts, with a little extra time. Picky guests: a wrong guess costs double time. At the desk, the twist is one of these. Packed in straw: every box needs two shakes. A long shelf: two more boxes. Careful hands: a wrong box costs double. Few shakes: only two shakes a letter before shakes cost points. The briefing repeats the twist.

The weekly challenge is the holiday double: Gift Sort shifts 4 to 6, then stack 4 at the Wish Desk, scored together like the story. The week's twist is on the belt. If the clock beats you in the workshop, the run ends there with the points you have.

The monthly challenge is the midwinter post: stacks 3, 4 and 5 at the Wish Desk, one after another, with every box packed in straw.

## Practice, tutorial and sound check

Practice rhythm is a night of Midnight Countdown where the party never fizzles and nothing is saved. Choose any night you have opened with Left and Right on its row of the title. The tutorial sorts two gifts on the belt, then answers a letter at the Wish Desk. Sound check lists the gameplay cues and lets you play them, so you can learn each gift's shake and each cue before you play. Help is this guide.

## Records and results

The Gift Sort, Wish Desk and Midnight Countdown rows on the title name your best for the chosen shift, stack or night, and Play, story mode names your best story score. Records reads every best: the story, the blizzard story, Gift Sort, the Wish Desk and each stack, Muddled post, Midnight Countdown and each night, how many are open, and your challenge results. When a run ends you hear its results, then the results menu. Next stack or Next night plays the next one. Play again or Try again plays the same run. Hear the results again reads them once more, and Back to the title goes back. Escape on the results menu does the same as Back to the title.

## The title

The title has these rows. Play, story mode. Blizzard story, which says locked until you finish story mode. Gift Sort, the Wish Desk and Midnight Countdown, where Left and Right choose the shift, stack or night. Muddled post. Challenges. Practice rhythm. Tutorial, Help and Sound check. Records, Settings, and Back to the parlor. On your first visit the title starts on Tutorial.

## Controls

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's spoken instructions follow your bindings.

In Gift Sort:

Space: start the belt, then shake the gift.

Left arrow: send the gift to the person on your left.

Up arrow: send it to the person straight ahead.

Right arrow: send it to the person on your right.

Down arrow: drop it in the return sack, from shift 3.

R: repeat the wishes.

S: the gift on the belt and the clock. Press it again straight away for the shift, the gifts sorted and the score.

H: a hint about the gift you just shook, for half its points.

At the Wish Desk:

Space: open the first letter, then shake the box in front of you.

Hold Space: wrap the box for the letter.

Left and Right arrows: walk the shelf.

Up and Down arrows: jump to the first or last box.

R: read the letter again.

S: where you stand and the letter. Press it again straight away for the stack and the score.

H: a hint, for half the letter's points.

In Midnight Countdown:

Space: play the bell, and start the music when it waits.

Down arrow: play the drum.

Up arrow: play the horn.

R: say the last call in words, with its beats.

S: the minutes to midnight, the party energy, the score and the combo.

H: list your instruments.

Everywhere:

F1: help for what you are doing right now. F2 stops speech.

Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title is Leave the challenge. When you come back from the pause menu in the middle of the music, a bar of ticks counts you back in and the band plays the call again. Notes you already answered stay answered.
