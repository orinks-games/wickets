# Winter Wonderland

## About the game

Winter holidays: sort gifts by sound, then keep midnight on the beat. Gift Sort: clear the shift before the clock runs out. Midnight Countdown: match the band to hold energy until midnight, or the party fizzles.

## The two parts

Winter Wonderland has two parts. Midnight Countdown happens on New Year's Eve: the band plays a short rhythm and you answer it on the beat, as bells, drums and horns join in, all the way to midnight. Story mode plays three Gift Sort shifts and then a night of Midnight Countdown, and scores them together.

## Gift Sort: the belt

Before each shift you hear the briefing: how many gifts to sort, how long the shift clock runs, and who is waiting. Each person stands on your left, straight ahead or on your right, and wishes for one thing. Press Space to start the belt. A soft thump means a gift has landed on the belt. It rides towards the edge. Quick beeping means it is nearly there, and if it rides off it breaks and the clock loses 4 seconds.

## Gift Sort: shaking and sending

Space shakes the gift. Listen: blocks rattle, a teddy bear squishes, teacups clink, bells jingle. Later shifts bring crinkling sweets, sloshing snow globes, ticking clocks, squeaky toys, thumping books and chiming music boxes. Send the gift with the Left arrow for the person on your left, the Up arrow for the person straight ahead and the Right arrow for the person on your right. Rising bells from their side mean they are delighted. A low buzz means they did not wish for it: the clock loses a few seconds and the gift stays on the belt for another try. From shift 3, some gifts are wanted by nobody: drop those in the return sack behind you with the Down arrow. In the first two shifts, the game also names each sound a moment after you shake.

## Gift Sort: shifts and scoring

There are 8 shifts. Each one has more gifts, more kinds of sound and a faster belt, and the belt speeds up as you sort. The first three briefings say what each wish sounds like. From shift 4 you must work it out. From shift 5, someone changes their mind half way through: a doorbell rings and you hear their new wish. A delivery scores more the sooner you make it and the longer your streak, times the shift number. Clearing a shift adds a bonus for the time left on the clock. Shifts you reach stay open: choose a starting shift with Left and Right on the Gift Sort row of the title.

## Midnight Countdown: call and answer

The countdown starts at ten minutes to midnight. Each minute, the band plays a call one bar long, then you answer it in the next bar: the same instruments on the same beats. A soft clock ticks every beat, with a lower tock on the first beat of each bar, so you can count along. Bells are Space. At seven minutes to midnight the drums join, a deep boom, answered with the Down arrow. At four minutes the horns join, a bright brass blast, answered with the Up arrow. The music stops while each new instrument is introduced, and waits for you to press Space. You can try the drum and horn while it waits. In the final seconds the full orchestra plays one long two-bar call with every instrument, for double points.

## Midnight Countdown: scoring and energy

A note answered right on the beat is perfect. A little early or late is good. Every answer in a row adds to your combo, and every 8 in a row raise the score multiplier, up to times 4, with rising chimes. A deflating party blower means a missed note and a short buzz means the wrong instrument. Both break the combo and drain the party's energy. A soft tap means you pressed when there was nothing to answer. If the energy runs out, the party fizzles before midnight. Reach midnight to hear the bells, the champagne and the fireworks, and to open the next night. There are 5 nights, each faster with trickier rhythms. From the third night some notes fall between the beats.

## Story mode

Story mode plays shifts 1 to 3 of Gift Sort, a short bridge, then the first night of Midnight Countdown. Your story score is both parts added together. Finish the story once to open the blizzard story: shifts 4 to 6 and the third night, the village square.

## Challenges

Choose Challenges on the title, or Challenges in the parlor's lobby, for three runs that are the same for every player. Each row says what the challenge is, then whether you have played it, your best and your streak. The briefing states the twist and how long until the next one before you play. Your result counts when the run ends, win or lose. Choosing Leave the challenge on the pause menu does not count. Escape on a briefing goes back without starting. Afterwards you hear your score, your best on that challenge and your streak, then the results menu offers the same challenge again or Back to the challenges.

The daily challenge alternates. On even days of the month it is the rush: one Gift Sort shift at shift 5. On odd days it is the Midnight warm-up: one night of Midnight Countdown at the village square. Each day brings one twist. On the belt, the twist is one of these. Rush hour: the belt runs a quarter faster. Short clock: four fifths of the usual time. Big order: three extra gifts, with a little extra time. Picky guests: a wrong guess costs double time. At the party, the twist is one of these. Up-tempo: the band plays faster. Fragile party: misses drain half again as much energy. Tight timing: answer closer to the beat. Late arrivals: the party starts with 35 energy instead of 60. The Gift Sort briefing and the countdown's first intro repeat the twist.

The weekly challenge is the holiday double: Gift Sort shifts 4 to 6, then New Year's Eve on the snowy rooftop, scored together like the story. The week's twist is on the belt. If the clock beats you in the workshop, the run ends there with the points you have.

The monthly challenge is the Midnight Gala, a New Year special: a grand organ and brass finale rings at midnight. Its rule: the full orchestra plays from the very first call, so bells, drums and horns can all turn up from ten minutes to midnight, and the music never stops to introduce them. The party starts at full energy, at the village square's tempo.

## Practice, tutorial and sound check

Practice rhythm is a night of Midnight Countdown where the party never fizzles and nothing is saved. Choose any night you have opened with Left and Right on its row of the title. The tutorial explains both parts a page at a time. The sound check plays every sound in the game with its name, so you can learn each gift's shake and each cue before you play. Help is this guide.

## Records and results

The Gift Sort and Midnight Countdown rows on the title name your best for the chosen shift or night, and Play, story mode names your best story score. Records reads every best: the story, the blizzard story, Gift Sort, Midnight Countdown and each night, how many shifts and nights are open, and your challenge results. When a run ends you hear its results, then the results menu. Next night, after a night you won, plays the next night. Play again or Try again plays the same run. Hear the results again reads them once more, and Back to the title goes back. Escape on the results menu does the same as Back to the title.

## The title

The title has these rows. Play, story mode. Blizzard story, which says locked until you finish story mode. Gift Sort and Midnight Countdown, where Left and Right choose the shift or night. Challenges. Practice rhythm. Tutorial, Help and Sound check. Records, Settings, and Back to the parlor. On your first visit the title starts on Tutorial.

## Controls

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's spoken instructions follow your bindings.

In Gift Sort:

Space: start the belt, then shake the gift.

Left arrow: send the gift to the person on your left.

Up arrow: send it to the person straight ahead.

Right arrow: send it to the person on your right.

Down arrow: drop it in the return sack, from shift 3.

R: repeat the wishes.

S: the gift on the belt and the clock. Press it again straight away for the shift, the gifts sorted and the score.

H: a hint about the gift you just shook, for half its points.

In Midnight Countdown:

Space: play the bell, and start the music when it waits.

Down arrow: play the drum.

Up arrow: play the horn.

R: say the last call in words, with its beats.

S: the minutes to midnight, the party energy, the score and the combo.

H: list your instruments.

Everywhere:

F1: help for what you are doing right now. F2 stops speech.

Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title is Leave the challenge. When you come back from the pause menu in the middle of the music, a bar of ticks counts you back in and the band plays the call again. Notes you already answered stay answered.
