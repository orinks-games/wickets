# Tightrope

## About the game

Walk a wire strung between rooftops, one careful step at a time, high above the city. Everything you need is in the sound: your balance is a tone, the wind warns you before it pushes, and pigeons coo from where they sit. Ten crossings make the skyline, each longer, higher and windier than the last.

## Your balance

A tone plays the whole time you are on the wire. When you are balanced it sits in the centre, low and steady. As you lean, the tone moves toward the side you lean to and climbs in pitch: the further you lean, the higher and further out it goes.

A lean grows by itself, the way a real wire tips you. Shift your weight with Left and Right. Press the opposite way to your lean: leaning left, press Right. Each press keeps you moving for a moment, so press briefly and give it time, or you will swing past the middle.

When you come back to balance after leaning, the steady chime sounds, a soft two-note chime: that is your moment to step. Lean hard and an alarm sounds from that side, and the game says "Leaning hard left" or "Leaning hard right". Lean all the way and you fall to the safety net.

The status key, S, always says your lean in words, so you never have to judge it by pitch alone.

## Walking

Space takes one step. Steps need a moment between them, and no foot lands quite square: every step jolts the wire a little, so settle after each one.

Step only when balanced. A step while you lean past the safe line is a wobble: the wire throws you further the way you were leaning. A refused step makes a soft double knock. A step is refused when it comes too soon after the last one, or while you crouch.

A chime sounds from the far rooftop every few seconds, straight ahead. It grows louder as you get near. The game also says when you are halfway, 3 steps out and 1 step out.

## Wind

Every gust is warned in time. First a whistle sounds from the side the wind will come from, and a voice says "Gust from the left" or "Gust from the right". Then a whoosh rises from that side for at least 2 seconds and peaks the moment the wind hits. The wind then pushes you away from that side for about a second.

You have two answers:

- Lean a little into the wind before it arrives: for a gust from the left, a press of Left.
- Crouch with Down. Crouching makes the wire much steadier and the wind pushes less than half as hard, but you cannot step. Stand with Up once it passes.

The status key tells you which side a gust comes from and how many seconds are left. The hint key, H, says what to do about it.

## Pigeons

From the second crossing on, pigeons land on the wire ahead of you, with a flutter of wings and a bounce of the wire toward their side. The game says how many steps ahead it sits and on which side, and you hear it coo from there, closer with every step.

Walk into a pigeon and it bursts up in your face and knocks you hard to the other side. Shoo it with Tab once it is within 2 steps, which dips the wire a little, or wait: a pigeon flies off by itself after several seconds. On the Practice wire it waits until you shoo it.

## Sway and height

Long wires sway, most at mid-span, back and forth in a steady rhythm. You feel it as a slow push one way, then the other, and hear it as the wire creaking faster. Creaks come from the side you lean to.

The street hums far below with the odd car horn and siren. The higher the wire, the quieter the street and the stronger the wind. These sounds are only atmosphere: nothing in them needs an answer.

## Falling

Lean all the way over and you fall: a rushing drop and the bounce of the safety net. You lose a life and climb back to the step where you fell, balanced, with a moment of calm before the wire moves again. Lose your last life and the run is over.

On the practice wire falling costs nothing.

## Scoring

- Each step scores 10 points times the crossing's number.
- Reaching the far rooftop scores 100 times the crossing's number.
- Finish under the par time for 5 points for every second to spare. Par is about 1.3 seconds a step, plus 6 seconds.
- A crossing with no falls scores a bonus of 50 times its number.

Your best score on each mode, your best time on each crossing and your furthest crossing are saved. Records, on the title, reads them all, with your challenge results.

## Modes

- Play, skyline run: all 10 crossings with 3 lives, from the bakery to the Skyline Spire.
- Night skyline: locked until you finish all 10 crossings. The same wires, twitchier, with harder wind and busier pigeons. Gusts are still always warned.
- Crossing select: walk one crossing alone, for its best time. A crossing opens once you reach it.
- Practice wire: a short, low wire where the gust and the pigeon are each explained as they arrive. On your first visit the title starts on it.

Before each run a page says what the run is. Enter begins, and Escape goes back to the title.

## After a run

When a run ends you hear how it went and what records it set. Then the results menu offers Play again, or Try again after a fall ends the run, Hear the results again, and Back to the title. After a single crossing you win, Next crossing comes first. After a challenge, the last row is Back to the challenges. Escape does the same as the last row.

## Challenges

Challenges are the same wires for every player, built from the date.

- Daily: one crossing with a twist, and 3 lives. Twists include Gale day, with gusts twice as often, Pigeon parade, with 3 times the pigeons, Loose wire, with twice the sway, and Slick wire, where every step slips a little to one side.
- Weekly: 3 high crossings in a row, with a twist, on only 2 lives.
- Monthly: the Grand Span, one immense wire 60 floors up and 60 steps long, with a single life, and the wind changes side with every gust.

The rule and the time until the next challenge are read before you start, and Escape on that briefing goes back without starting. Any finished run counts toward your streak, even one that ends in the net. Leave the challenge, on the pause menu, does not. Afterwards you hear your score, your best and your streak.

## Sound check

Sound check, on the title, plays each sound you need on the wire with its name and meaning. Its first row demonstrates the balance tone: balanced, then leaning further and further left, then right, with each lean named.

## Controls

- Left and Right: shift your weight.
- Space: take a step.
- Down: crouch. Up: stand.
- Tab: shoo a pigeon within 2 steps.
- S: your step, lean, and any gust or pigeon. Press it again straight away for lives, score, time on the wire and which wire you are on.
- R: repeat the last announcement.
- H: a hint for this moment.
- Escape: pause. The pause menu can resume, open settings or this help, go back to the title, or go back to the lobby. During a challenge, its leave row is Leave the challenge.
- F1: help for the screen you are on.

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's spoken instructions follow your bindings.
