## About the game

You are a bike courier weaving through the busy streets of Courier City, inspired by Cyclepath by Talon. Parked cars line the curb on your right, traffic rolls up from behind, and open doors, potholes, curbs and ramps keep you on your toes. Take on time trials, push your distance in the endless ride, make timed drops against the clock, or grab deliveries from the job board to earn cash for new bikes and districts.

Distances and speeds here are in metric. Switch Units to US in Settings if you prefer feet, miles and miles an hour, which puts the safe turn speed at 13 miles an hour.

## The street

Every ride runs down a single city street. Parked cars line the curb on your right, with the bike lane beside them. The first metre of the bike lane is the door zone, where a parked car door can swing open in front of you.

A painted line separates the bike lane from the traffic lane, which stretches out to the centre line. You can steer anywhere between the parked cars and the centre line, and you cannot cross either edge.

## Riding

Hold Up arrow to pedal and Down arrow to brake. Your brakes build up over a moment, so give yourself room to slow down. Let go of both to coast.

Hold Left arrow or Right arrow to steer across the street. Hold Tab while pedalling to sprint for extra speed until you run out of breath, and your stamina recovers while you ride without sprinting.

## Hops and jumps

Press Space to hop while you are moving. The BMX hops about 40 centimetres, plenty to clear a curb. The road bike and e-bike only hop about 10 centimetres, so steer around curbs on those. The moped cannot hop at all, so you need to ride around curbs and potholes.

Press Space right at a ramp's lip to launch into a jump and earn airtime points.

## Hazards

Every hazard warns you at least 1.5 seconds before you reach it, scaled to your speed, from the side it sits on.

A parked car's door can swing open into the door zone, so steer left out of the door zone before you reach it.

A pothole's warning speeds up as you close in. Hitting one gives the road bike or e-bike a flat tyre, slowing you down until you stop and stand still for 3 seconds to swap the tube. The BMX rolls right through potholes, only losing a bit of speed.

Raised curbs run across the bike lane, and ramps give you a chance to jump. When the urgent warning goes off, a hazard is right in your line, so hop, brake or steer.

## Cars from behind

Cars roll up from behind and pass on your left whenever there is room. Hold your line while a car goes by, because steering into it causes a crash.

If you are riding in the middle of the traffic lane, a car cannot get past and honks at you. Pull over to the right to clear the way, and you earn a tip.

## Junction turns

Each junction turn is called out ahead of time with its distance. Move into the bike lane for a right turn, or out near the centre line for a left turn. Either way, slow down to 22 kilometres an hour or less before the junction line, or you will run wide and lose speed.

## Crashes

Crashing into an open door, a passing car, or a curb on the road bike or e-bike knocks you down for 3 seconds. You get back on your bike in the bike lane, clear of the door zone, and each crash costs 3000 ride points.

## The endless ride

In the endless ride, you keep going until your third crash. The farther you get, the closer hazards pack together and the more often oncoming cars show up. Your best distance in each district is saved.

Parcels sit along the route, and you grab them by riding over them at 20 kilometres an hour or less. Go any faster and you sail right past. Shop doorway parcels sit in the door zone and pay 3 dollars, while parcel lockers on the traffic island by the centre line sit out in traffic and pay 6 dollars. You keep all the cash you pick up, even if you end the ride early.

## Oncoming cars

During the endless ride, oncoming cars overtake toward you in the traffic lane. Each one warns from ahead at least 1.5 seconds before it reaches you, so steer right into the bike lane until it passes. An urgent warning means it is headed straight for your line.

## Against the clock

Make delivery drops to keep the clock alive. You start with 60 seconds in your chosen district, with the first drop right near the start. There is no crash limit and no finish line: the ride ends when the clock hits zero.

Drops work like parcels in the endless ride: roll over one at 20 kilometres an hour or less to complete it. A shop doorway drop in the door zone adds 14 seconds and pays 3 dollars, while a parcel locker by the centre line adds 18 seconds and pays 6 dollars. Make a drop within 30 seconds of the last one for a quick drop bonus of 4 extra seconds. The clock caps at 90 seconds.

After each drop, you get the time added and the time remaining. Crashing costs 8 seconds off the clock on top of the 3 seconds you spend getting back up. You get calls at 30 seconds and 10 seconds left, and the clock ticks once a second from 15 seconds down, then twice as fast from 5 seconds down.

Every drop has clear road before and after it, so it never overlaps with a hazard warning, though hazards still tighten up the farther you go. If you are on the moped, start braking hard for every drop.

Your score is the total number of drops you make. Your best count in each district is saved, and you keep every dollar you earn even if you quit early.

## The job board

The job board lists three courier deliveries across the districts you own, showing each route, distance and pay in dollars. Beat the deadline for full pay, or arrive late for half pay. Finishing a delivery brings up a fresh set of three jobs.

## The bike shop and districts

Spend your cash in the bike shop to unlock new bikes and districts. Anything you buy becomes active right away, and you can switch anytime later from Bike select or District select in the garage.

The mountain bike rides on knobbly tyres that roll through potholes, hop curbs and hold speed over cobbles. The e-bike adds motor assist up to 25 kilometres an hour. The moped trades pedals for an engine that reaches 50 kilometres an hour, though it cannot hop or sprint.

Riverside's wet streets give your brakes less grip, so you need more room to stop. Old Town's cobbles slow down bikes with thin road tyres.

## Status, hint and call-outs

Press S for a quick status check with danger first: any close hazard or oncoming car, a flat tyre, then your speed and lane. Press S a second time right away for the rest, including what else is ahead, your distance from the parked cars or centre line, the clock, your stamina, and in the endless ride your distance, cash and crashes left. Against the clock, the first press leads with time left and the second gives your drops and cash.

Press H to find out what the next hazard is, how soon you will reach it, and what to do. Press R to repeat the last line.

Spoken call-outs, found in Settings from the title or pause menu, controls how often the game names upcoming hazards. Once per session is the default, naming each kind the first time it appears after you start Wicket's. Every time names them all, and Off stays quiet while keeping S and H available.

## Pace

The street pauses while a spoken line finishes, holding your bike, traffic, hazards and the clock in place. If you hold a key to pedal, brake, steer or sprint, the street keeps rolling right through speech. Repeating a line over and over stops holding the street after a while. This works at every pace and in every mode, though time spent waiting for speech still counts toward your ride time and ride points.

Pace in Settings controls how fast the street runs: relaxed, normal or quick. Normal is the default and sits a little slower than quick. Quick is full speed, and relaxed runs at half speed, scaling your bike, traffic, hazard warnings and the clock together. Changes take effect on your next ride.

Relaxed best times, ride points, distances and drops are saved on their own boards, including challenges and special rides. Normal and quick share boards, and cash, lessons, bikes and districts count at every pace.

## Modes

The title opens with the endless ride. More rides holds the time trial, Against the clock and Free ride. The job board appears on the title once you finish your first ride. Events holds the special rides, and the garage holds Bike select, District select and the bike shop.

Practice lessons: five short guided rides covering pedalling and coasting, braking, steering, the door zone and hopping. Each lesson gives you a goal and resets if you miss it.

Time trial: race the clock on three Downtown courses, Station Run, Market Street and Bank Square, using your chosen bike. Traffic is identical every run so you can learn the route. Pick a course from Time trial under More rides, and swap bikes in the garage.

Endless ride: ride through your chosen district until your third crash while grabbing parcels along the way.

Against the clock: keep the timer alive by making delivery drops in your chosen district.

Job board: timed courier deliveries for cash.

Free ride: 3 kilometres of Downtown with no clock, ideal for getting a feel for the street.

## Scoring and best times

Every course saves your best time on each bike. Ride points reward speed: finishing at par time earns 100000 points, and beating par earns more. A crash-free ride adds a 5000 point bonus, each second of airtime adds 1000, and each tip adds 1000. Every crash subtracts 3000 points.

## Challenges

Every player rides the exact same challenge courses. Morning run is a new 700 metre course each day, alternating between the road bike and the BMX by weekday. Rush hour is a new 1200 metre course every Monday in heavy traffic. Cross-town ride is a new 2200 metre course every month with 3 junction turns. Your best ride points count, and finishing a ride keeps your streak alive. Choosing Leave the challenge from the pause menu does not count.

## Special rides

Two special rides live under Events on the title and open only on their own days. Each saves its own best time and ride points per bike, separate from the regular time trial boards. Once an event closes, Play again on its results screen will not start a new run.

Leaf season ride runs from 17 October to the end of November, and stays locked under Events the rest of the year. It is 1000 metres of autumn Downtown, identical all season for every rider, with leaf piles sitting in the middle of the bike lane. Each pile signals its spot once the hazards ahead of it are behind you. Ride through a pile for 1000 ride points; leaf piles are never hazards, and hopping goes right over them. S counts the piles you have hit, and H names the next one.

Car-free Sunday opens on the third Sunday of every month with a 1200 metre course seeded from that day's date, shared by every rider, on streets closed to cars. On other days it stays locked under Events and shows when it opens next.

## Controls

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's instructions follow your bindings.

- Up arrow: pedal, while held. On the moped, it opens the throttle.
- Down arrow: brake, while held.
- Left arrow and Right arrow: steer across the road, while held.
- Space: hop, or jump at a ramp's lip.
- Tab: sprint, while held with Up arrow.
- S: status. Press it twice for the clock and stamina.
- H: hint for the next hazard.
- R: repeat the last line.
- Escape: pause.
- F1: help for this screen.
- F2: stop speech.

On a controller, the d-pad steers, pedals and brakes, A hops, X sprints and Y gives the status.
