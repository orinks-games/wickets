# Nevermore Manor

## About the game

The front doors of Nevermore Manor have locked behind you, and the only way out lies a hundred floors down at the heart of the house. Each floor is a chain of haunted rooms where you solve a puzzle to unlock the exit, freeze in place when a ghost drifts past, and watch your step for traps.

Every floor is built from its number, so floor 40 is the same layout for every player, every time you visit it. As you descend through ten wings, Floor select and the housekeeper's diary open up to tell the story of the house.

## Rooms and walls

Each room is a grid of floorboards, five cells across on the upper floors and up to nine near the bottom of the house. You always face north, and bumping into a wall leaves you where you stand. Press S to check how many steps away the exit door is, or press S twice for your exact column from the west wall and row from the south wall.

The door behind you locks as soon as you step inside, so there is no going back. Once you solve the room's puzzle, the exit unlocks on another wall. Stand in the cell next to the open door and walk through it, and on the last room of a floor you will step onto the stairs down.

## Finding things

Objects lie scattered around the rooms and call out from where they sit: a clock, a crystal, a potion, a music box, a bell, a candle, a raven, keys and a heart. Left is west, right is east, ahead is north and behind is south. The closer you get to something, the stronger and more frequent its calls become, and stepping onto an object names it.

Sound check on the title plays every cue you need in the manor by name, including a quick demo of left, right, near and far.

## Charms

Starting on floor 3, some rooms hold a helpful charm. It calls out from where it sits, and walking onto it picks it up at no cost.

- A pinch of salt wards off one catch from a ghost, the shadow or the founder, letting you stay right where you are. You can carry up to 3 pinches, and they stay with you for the whole run.
- A candle stub adds 20 seconds to the haunt clock.

Press S twice at any time to check how much salt you are carrying.

## The puzzles

- Find: the room names the object you need. Walk onto it and press Space to pick it up.
- Match: a phonograph plays a clue without naming it. Find the matching object in the room and press Space on it.
- Riddle: a voice gives you a riddle describing the right object. Work out which one it is, then walk onto it and press Space.
- Offering, from floor 2: a spirit seals the exit and wants something you already picked up on this floor. Use Tab to choose it, stand at the exit door and press Space to offer it.
- Missing, from floor 6: the phonograph plays every object in the room except one. Find the object it left out and press Space on it.
- Séance, from floor 12: a medium calls up objects one after another without naming them, two at first, three from floor 40 and four from floor 75. Pick them up in that exact order.

Rooms also hold decoys alongside the real answer, with more of them deeper down. Picking up or offering the wrong thing costs time on the haunt clock, and that penalty grows on deeper floors. Press R whenever you want to replay the room's clue.

## Ghosts

Ghosts roam the halls, each moving at its own pace. The weeping widow drifts slowly, the chained butler moves quickly from floor 5, and the whispering child stays faint until she is close from floor 10. A ghost approaches from far off, growing closer from its direction.

When a ghost is right beside you, freeze and do not take a single step until it drifts away. Picking things up, switching what you hold and checking status are all quiet enough; only footsteps give you away.

If you move while a ghost passes, it catches you, costing one of your three courage and sending you back to the entrance door. Lose all three courage on a floor and you flee the house. Every ghost you let pass safely scores points.

From floor 25, the blind hound joins the hunt, and it works in reverse. It finds you by your stillness, so when it warns you to keep walking, stay on the move until it has passed.

On the first four floors, a voice calls out to freeze when a ghost arrives. After floor 4, you rely on the warning alone, though S always reports whether a ghost is approaching or passing.

## The deeper dangers

Each new danger arrives on its own floor, and the house introduces it in full the first time you meet it. The first room of every floor is always safe from them.

- Rotten boards, from floor 8: a board groans when you stand beside it. Stepping on it drops you through the floor, costing one courage, sending you back to the door and leaving an impassable hole behind.
- Twists, from floor 11: floors can carry twists that change the rules for that floor, and deeper floors can stack up to two at once by floor 71.
- Dark rooms, from floor 15: objects only call out when you are three steps away or closer, so sweep the room row by row.
- The shadow, from floor 20: a dark presence stalks the room one cell at a time, moving toward your footsteps. Stand still and it wanders off, but if it reaches you or you walk into it, it catches you. Press S to check how many steps away it is.
- Mimics, from floor 35: fake copies of the answer hop to a new spot every few seconds, while the real object never moves. Touching a mimic costs time just like a decoy.
- Mirrored rooms, from floor 45: east and west flip sides, putting east on your left and west on your right, while north and south stay normal.
- The pendulum, from floor 51: a blade swings back and forth along one row of the room, cutting you for one courage if it hits your cell. Cross its row right after it passes, and press S to check which row it is in.
- Deep water, from floor 61: flooded cells slow you down for a moment after each step, and every splash draws the next ghost sooner.
- Falling stones, from floor 71: dust trickles onto your cell, and 2 seconds later a stone drops there. Step off as soon as the dust starts. Stones never fall while a ghost is passing.
- The founder, from floor 81: the first Nevermore enters each room behind you and hunts you down, swinging blows you can counter with your iron candlestick.
- The shifting house, from floor 91: the exit door sometimes moves to another wall. The house tells you which wall it moved to, and S gives the new distance.

## The founder

From floor 81, the founder walks the halls: the first Nevermore, risen from his grave in the crypt. About 15 seconds after you enter a room, he comes through the door behind you and marches toward you one step at a time. Freezing does not stop him, and he follows you into every room on the floor.

You carry an iron candlestick to fight back. When he reaches you, he stops and winds up a blow. Wait until halfway through his wind-up, then press Space to strike him and knock him 2 steps back. Strike too early and he blocks it, so step away before his blow lands or you lose one courage and flee back to the entrance.

He takes 3 hits from floor 81, 4 from floor 91 and 5 in Endless night. Bring him down and he crumbles to dust for the rest of the floor, earning 200 points times the floor number. Press S to check where he is and whether he is winding up.

## The haunt clock

Every floor gives you a haunt clock to beat: nearly four minutes on floor 1, longer on floors with more rooms or longer séances, and tighter as you go deeper. Warnings mark one minute, thirty seconds and ten seconds left, and if the clock runs out the house keeps you.

Press H for a hint that points toward what you need. A hint costs five seconds of the clock, rising to ten seconds from floor 20 and fifteen from floor 60.

## Pace

The haunt clock and the ghosts pause while a room introduction or another line is being spoken, though asking for the same line over and over stops pausing after a while. This works at every pace and in every mode. Time spent during speech still counts toward your floor time and comes off your time bonus.

You can set Pace in Settings to relaxed, normal or quick. Normal is the default and a bit steadier than quick, quick is the fastest, and relaxed runs at half speed. The haunt clock, ghosts and every danger follow your chosen pace.

Changing pace takes effect on your next run, so an active climb, challenge or Halloween night finishes at the pace it started on.

Relaxed scores and best times are tracked on their own boards, including challenges and events, while normal and quick share their boards. Cleared floors, deepest floors, candles and unlocks count at every pace.

## Modes

- Play: climb from your highest open floor, floor after floor, until the house stops you or you clear floor 100. Each floor starts with a fresh clock, full courage and empty pockets. Every floor you clear stays cleared, so leaving from the pause menu between floors loses nothing.
- Floor select: pick a wing and replay any open floor on its own to beat your best time. Clearing a floor unlocks the next one.
- Nightmare: unlocks when you clear floor 25. Take on the whole house from floor 1 with a quarter less time, faster and more frequent ghosts, at least three kinds from the start and no freeze call. Your courage lasts the whole run, with one restored on every tenth landing on floors 11, 21 and so on.
- Endless night: unlocks when you clear floor 100. Descend past the heart of the house from floor 101 without end, with each floor tougher than the last and up to three twists at once. Courage lasts the run, with one restored every five floors.

## Halloween

The manor follows the real calendar. For two weeks before Halloween, starting on 17 October, the house stirs as Lantern Jack roams the halls blowing out candles. A row on the title called The house stirs lets you play a haunted floor with restless ghosts, and each floor you clear lights one of 13 candles. You can light more than one candle a day, so starting late still leaves plenty of time to light all 13.

From 30 October to 1 November, The Lantern Night opens on the title. It is 13 floors in a row from floor 1 with the lights out and restless ghosts, where your courage lasts the whole night and refills at every fifth landing. Every candle you lit adds 10 seconds to each haunt clock. Clear all 13 floors to keep Lantern Jack's ember for the year. Leaving early ends the attempt, and the next try starts back at floor 1. Outside those dates, both rows stay locked on the title and tell you when they open, while Records tracks your candles and embers under Halloween.

## Scoring and records

Each solved room scores 100 points times the floor number, and each ghost you evade scores 25 times the floor number. In the midnight masquerade, rooms score as floor 6. Clearing a floor adds 5 points for every second left on the clock plus 100 for each courage you kept.

The manor saves your best time on every floor, your deepest floor in Nightmare and Endless night, and your top scores in every mode. Choose Records on the title to hear them all, wing by wing, alongside your challenge results.

## Unlocks and curses

Choose Unlocks on the title to browse everything you have earned, grouped by category. Press Enter to use an unlocked entry, or press Enter or F1 on a locked one to learn how to earn it.

- Modes: Nightmare and Endless night.
- Curses: optional twists you can switch on yourself after clearing a floor that carries that twist. Press Enter to toggle a curse on or off. Each active curse adds a quarter to your score in Play, Floor select, Nightmare and Endless night, without affecting challenges.
- Diary pages: twenty pages of the housekeeper's diary, earned one for every five floors you clear. Press Enter to read a page and piece together the house's story.
- Bestiary: every ghost and danger you have encountered. Press Enter to hear its entry and how to survive it.
- Music: the manor's soundtrack, unlocked as you go deeper. Press Enter to play a track.
- Feats: tough milestones, such as clearing a five-room floor without ever getting caught.

Whenever you earn something new, the game announces it at the end of the floor.

## Results

When a run ends, the results pages walk through how it went: the outcome, your score and record, how many ghosts you evaded and your floor times. From the results menu you can choose Play again, or Try again after a loss, Hear the results again or Back to the title. After clearing a single floor, Next floor sits at the top so you can jump right into the next one. After a challenge, the menu lets you replay it or return to Challenges, and Escape acts like the last row.

## Challenges

Challenges on the title give every player in the world the exact same house, with the same rooms and objects in the same places. Each row lists the challenge, your best score, whether you have played it and your streak. A quick briefing explains the twist and how long until the next challenge, and pressing Enter on the last page starts the run while Escape backs out. Finishing a run, whether by escaping, running out of time or losing your courage, records your score and streak; choosing Leave the challenge from the pause menu does not count it. Challenges never overwrite your best times or open floors, though they still earn unlocks.

- Daily, Tonight's haunting: one floor between floors 3 and 30 with a twist, refreshed every day.
- Weekly, The long night: three floors in a row starting between floors 10 and 40 with a twist, where your three courage must last the whole climb. Refreshed every Monday.
- Monthly, The midnight masquerade: a hidden ballroom wing open only for this event, with five rooms, every ghost abroad, a longer clock and mostly séances. Refreshed every month.

The twists you can meet are:

- Restless dead: ghosts arrive twice as often, with at least three kinds roaming.
- Lights out: objects call out more faintly and less often.
- Witching hour: the haunt clock is two fifths shorter.
- Cursed hands: picking up or offering the wrong object costs double time.
- Silent spirits: hints are disabled.
- Pitch dark, on floors 15 and deeper: every room is dark.
- Hall of mirrors, on floors 45 and deeper: every room is mirrored.

Challenges draw from the first five twists. Active twists are announced at the start of each floor, and F1 repeats them. Your streak counts how many days, weeks or months in a row you have finished that challenge.

## Tips

- Pause before you move to get your bearings, then walk until the object is straight ahead or right under your feet.
- If two things seem identical, walk to each one and check its name. If one hops away while you move, it is a mimic.
- Check for rotten boards before stepping forward, and walk around any board that groans beside you.
- When the shadow closes in, stand still and let it wander away, unless the blind hound is passing.
- When a ghost approaches, finish your step and freeze until it drifts past. For the blind hound, do the opposite and keep walking.
- Against the founder, wait for the second half of his wind-up before striking; swinging early wastes the hit and leaves you open.
- Grab every charm you come across, especially salt for the deeper floors.

## Controls

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's instructions follow your bindings.

- Up, Down, Left and Right arrows: walk one step north, south, west or east. You always face north.
- Space: pick up the object you are standing on, offer what you hold at a sealed door, check the door or strike the founder when he is beside you.
- Tab: cycle through the objects you carry.
- I: list everything in your pockets. This key belongs to this game alone and cannot be changed in Settings.
- S: short status covering nearby dangers, the haunt clock, your courage and how many steps to the exit door.
- S twice: full status covering the floor and room, dark or mirrored state, other dangers, the puzzle, your column and row, what you hold, your salt and your score.
- R: repeat the room's instructions and replay its clue.
- H: get a hint pointing toward what you need, at the cost of a few seconds on the haunt clock.
- F1: context help for what you are doing right now.
- Escape: pause the game to resume, change settings, read this guide, return to the title or leave a challenge.
