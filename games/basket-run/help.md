# Basket Run

## About the game

Easter morning: eggs tumble over the meadow. Catch them in your basket, bank them at the cart before the bell, and dodge mud clods and bees. A voice can call out every drop.

## The meadow and your lanes

The meadow is a row of lanes, numbered from 1 on the far left. The first rounds have three lanes, then four, then five. You step one lane at a time with the Left and Right arrows. Each step rustles the grass and says your lane. At either edge you bump the fence.

You hear the meadow from your own lane: a drop straight ahead is in your lane, one a little to the left is one lane left, and one far to the left is further. Breeze and birdsong play quietly behind it all.

## The cart

The cart sits at one edge: left on odd rounds, right on even (weekly marathon too). Endless, daily, festival, and practice keep it on the left. Soft bell from its lane; Tab rings it and says how far. Wood block when you step into its lane.

Standing in the cart's lane, press Space to bank your basket: the eggs roll into the cart and become points. Banking anywhere else tells you where the cart is.

## What falls

Everything that falls makes its own sound from its own lane, and falls from high to low: the lower the pitch, and the louder it gets, the sooner it lands. Every drop gives you at least 1.6 seconds of warning, enough to cross the whole meadow.

- An egg is a soft whistle. Be in its lane when it lands and it drops into your basket with a woody thump. Miss it and it cracks on the grass.
- A golden egg sparkles like little bells. It is worth five eggs.
- A mud clod is a low, gloopy wobble. If it lands in your basket it spoils every egg inside. If it misses you, it thuds into the grass.
- A bee is a buzz. If it lands on you it stings, and a basket breaks. If it misses you, it buzzes away.

The first time you meet each of them, the game stops and explains it before it falls.

## The basket

The basket holds four eggs in the first rounds, five from round 3, and six in the last two. When it fills, three wooden knocks tell you. An egg that lands in a full basket bounces out and cracks, so bank before it fills up again.

## Points and streaks

An egg is worth 10 points and a golden egg 50. Every egg caught in a row adds a tenth to the next one's value, up to double after a streak of ten. A cracked egg, mud in the basket or a sting breaks the streak. Banking a full basket earns 25 more. Clearing a round earns 50 times the round number, and 100 more if no egg cracked.

Spoken call-outs is a row in Settings, from the title or from the pause menu during a run. Play a whole run with it off, and everything you bank is worth a tenth more. Switching it on during a run ends the bonus for that run.

## Rounds and the clock

Each round opens with its announcement, then a chime and "Go". Nothing falls until the announcement has had time to be read. Press Space to start sooner. While a page is open in the middle of play, such as a first-time explanation or a round's summary, the meadow waits: only Enter goes on, so a late Space press cannot skip it. Each round has a clock. With ten seconds left a chime warns you, and the last five seconds tick. When the bell rings, eggs still in your basket are lost.

In the meadow, each round asks you to bank a share of its eggs: the round's opening announcement says how many. Bank too few and the run ends. You have 3 baskets: lose them all to bee stings and the run ends too.

The meadow has eight rounds. Round 2 brings mud clods and moves the cart to the right. Round 3 adds a fourth lane, golden eggs and bees. Round 4 sends some eggs in pairs, one close behind the other in a nearby lane. Round 5 adds a fifth lane, and bees that dive right behind an egg in the same lane: catch the egg, then step aside. Each round after is faster.

## Endless meadow

Clear all eight rounds to open the endless meadow: rounds without end, faster each time, with no egg target. Instead, every fifth cracked egg wears out a basket. Play until your baskets are gone.

## Easter morning

On Easter Sunday, and the day either side of it, the Easter morning row on the title opens a golden-egg hunt. It has three gentle rounds: sunrise in the garden on three lanes, then the orchard on four, then the big hunt on five. Golden eggs fall often and are worth double. There are no bees and no egg target, so the hunt always runs to the end. The results name how many golden eggs you found, and each Easter you play keeps a keepsake: your best hunt that year and the most golden eggs in one hunt. Records reads them all.

Easter moves from year to year, between the 22nd of March and the 25th of April. The rest of the year the row says locked, and F1 or Enter on it tells you how many days are left. In the ten days before Easter, Enter tells you how the Easter bunny is getting on.

## Challenges

Challenges on the title holds three runs that are the same for every player: a new daily each day, a new weekly each Monday and a new festival each month. All three turn over at midnight UTC. Each row says what the challenge is, then your best and your streak. F1 on a row reads its whole rule and when the next one comes. Choosing one reads its rule first. Enter starts the run. Escape goes back without starting.

- Daily, Twist of the day: one round of 60 seconds across five lanes, with no egg target, but every fifth cracked egg breaks a basket. Each day brings one twist, announced with a cuckoo call before the round and named in the round's opening. Golden rush: golden eggs three times as often. Bee swarm: no mud, twice the bees, more of them right behind an egg. Fast fall: everything falls as fast as the meadow ever allows. Mud slide: no bees, three times the mud. Small basket: the basket holds only 3.
- Weekly, The long meadow: a marathon of the meadow's rounds 4 to 8 back to back, each with its egg target, and the cart swapping sides every round. Your 3 baskets have to last all five rounds.
- Monthly, The Easter festival: three rounds across five lanes where the drops come in patterns. Pan pipes and a voice call each pattern as it starts:
  - An egg sweep drops an egg in every lane in turn, from one edge to the other. Get to the edge it starts from and run along with it.
  - A bee fence drops a bee in every lane but one, all at once, and the gap holds a golden egg. Find the gap.
  - A nest drops three eggs in one lane, then a mud clod right behind them. Catch the eggs, then step aside.

  A cascade of bells means you caught a pattern cleanly, for 100 bonus points. A low flop means it broke. Every fifth cracked egg breaks a basket. With Spoken call-outs off, the call names the pattern but not its lane.

Your result counts when a challenge run ends, however it ends: the results say your score, whether it is your best, your streak and when the next one comes. Choosing Leave the challenge on the pause menu does not count. You can replay a challenge as often as you like: the drops are the same, and only your best is kept. Finishing a challenge in each period in a row builds that challenge's streak.

## Practice lanes

Three slow lanes with every kind of drop, each explained the first time it falls and every drop called out. Nothing costs a basket and no score is saved. Pause and choose Back to the title to stop.

## The title

- Play, the meadow: the eight rounds, with your best score. The very first time, the tutorial opens first.
- Endless meadow: opens once the meadow is cleared. Until then the row says locked.
- Easter morning: the golden-egg hunt, open around Easter Sunday. The rest of the year the row says locked and counts down the days.
- Challenges: the daily twist, the weekly marathon and the monthly Easter festival, each with your best and streak.
- Practice lanes: try everything with nothing to lose.
- Tutorial: how to play, a page at a time. On your first visit the title starts here.
- Help: this guide.
- Sound check: each sound you need in play, by name. Falling sounds fall and land just as they do in play.
- Records: your best scores, the rounds you have cleared, your Easter keepsakes and your challenge results.
- Settings: speech, volume and controls, then this game's Spoken call-outs, on or off.
- Back to the parlor.

## Results

When a run ends you hear the whole result: your score, whether it is your best, and for a challenge your streak. Then the results menu offers Play again or Try again, Hear the results again, and Back to the title. After a challenge, the first row plays the same challenge again and the last row is Back to the challenges. Escape does the same as the last row.

## Controls

- Left and Right arrows: step one lane.
- Space: bank your basket at the cart. Before a round begins, Space starts it at once.
- Tab: ring the cart's bell and hear where it is.
- S: your lane, how full your basket is, and the next two things to land. Press it again straight away for the clock, eggs banked and needed, and baskets left, and a third time for your score and streak.
- R: repeat the last announcement.
- H: a hint for right now: which way to move, when to bank.
- F1: help for the screen you are on.
- Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title is Leave the challenge.

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's spoken instructions follow your bindings.
