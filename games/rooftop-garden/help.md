# Rooftop Garden

## About the game

City rooftop planters. Water, pick aphids, prune, and harvest by ear.

Every plant has a voice. Seeds are silent. Sprouts hum softly. As a plant grows it joins the garden's song, quietly at first and then in full voice once it blooms. All the parts are in one key, so the whole garden plays together.

The song changes as you play. The garden's own tune comes round about once a minute, and in between the garden makes up new tunes, playing each one twice. Every plant keeps its own instrument in every tune.

In your own garden, neglected plants wilt and fall silent, but watering brings them back. Overwatering slows growth for a while. You can recover from either mistake and keep tending the garden.

## Moving around

Each step plays a footstep and names the planter and what grows in it, for example "Row 1, planter 2: tomato, in bloom, thirsty." A hollow knock with the step means the planter is empty soil. At the edge of the garden the railing clanks.

Every plant's sounds come from its own planter, so a crackle or a buzz to your right is a planter to your right. You never need to place a sound by ear alone: S, H and L always tell you where things are.

## Planting

On an empty planter, press Space to open the seed packet. Up and Down choose a seed, Enter plants it, and Escape plants nothing. A new seed is already a little damp; keep it watered and it sprouts after part of a day.

You start with 3 seeds: tomato, squash and bean. Harvests in your own garden unlock more. Seed shed, on the title menu, lists every seed, its part in the song, and how many harvests unlock it.

- Tomato: a kalimba arpeggio. Thirsty, and ripens again and again.
- Squash: the bass. Slow and steady.
- Bean: plucked strings. The quickest grower.
- Sunflower: the flute melody. Unlocked by your first harvest.
- Lavender: the chimes. Happy in dry soil. Unlocked at 3 harvests.
- Mint: a cool, glassy pad. The thirstiest of all. Unlocked at 6 harvests.

## How plants grow

A plant goes from seed, to sprout, to growing, to in bloom, to ripe.

- A seed is silent.
- A sprout hums a soft tone from its planter.
- A growing plant plays its part of the song quietly.
- A plant in bloom plays its part in full voice.
- A ripe plant sparkles: press Tab to harvest it. It goes back to blooming and ripens again later.

Plants only grow while their soil is damp but not waterlogged, and they are free of aphids. A thirsty plant grows at half speed, and so does a leggy one. A seed counts as a plant here: keep it moist, and it calls you when it gets thirsty.

## What plants need

- Dry soil crackles; a thirsty plant's song droops. A soft falling tone speaks its name and planter—press Space to water.
- Watering moist soil squelches—now waterlogged. The plant gurgles, stops growing, and drops out of the garden song. Each extra watering keeps it soggy for longer. The soil drains by itself, faster on a sunny day, and you hear a drip and its name when it grows again. Water a plant when it is thirsty, not every time you pass it.
- Reviving. A plant left in bone-dry soil for a while wilts with a long falling sound and goes quiet. Nothing is lost: water it and it lifts its leaves again.
- Aphids. A buzz from a planter means aphids. The plant stops growing until you press Tab to pick them off.
- Pruning. A plant in bloom sometimes gets leggy, with a rustle, and grows slowly. Press Tab to prune it.

When something needs you, its name and planter are spoken, so you can press H to be pointed straight to it.

## Harmony

Different kinds of plant that are in bloom and well watered at the same time make a harmony: two kinds make a two-part harmony, three kinds a three-part harmony, and so on. A thirsty, waterlogged or wilted plant falls out of tune and leaves the harmony until you water it. S, for status, tells you which parts are singing. Bring all six kinds into tune at once for the full rooftop chorus.

## Days and weather

A garden day lasts about a minute and a half of play. Days only pass while you are in the garden, and the garden is saved when you leave, so it is waiting just as you left it.

Each morning brings new weather, announced with a short tune.

- Sunny: the soil dries quickly.
- Cloudy: the soil stays damp longer, and a shower may come.
- Breezy: the wind keeps aphids away, and you hear more wind and chimes.
- Showery: a rain shower is on its way.

A rain shower starts with distant thunder and waters every planter while it lasts, and even revives wilted plants. A bright two-note chime means it has passed.

## Growing your garden

Your garden starts with two rows of three planters, a squash already growing and a tomato seed just sown. At 4 harvests a neighbour lends you a third row, nearest you, and at 9 harvests a fourth planter joins the right of every row. New seeds unlock along the way.

The Play row on the title menu opens your garden and reads its day, plants and harvests, and your fullest harmony. Records, on the title menu, reads your bests, your seeds and your challenge results.

If you ever want a bare rooftop again, Start the garden over, near the end of the title menu, clears your garden after asking first. Your best records stay. Before you have a garden, the row says there is nothing to clear.

## Calendar events

Two touches of the real calendar visit your own garden. Neither changes how anything grows, thirsts or scores, and challenge gardens never see them.

- Pumpkin seeds: from 22 September to Thanksgiving, the seed packet has a pumpkin, after your other seeds. A pumpkin grows exactly like a squash and takes the squash's part in the song, but sings it as a low bassoon. It counts as the squash part in a harmony. A pumpkin you plant stays a pumpkin after the season ends, but once it ends the packet has no more pumpkin seeds, even if your garden was open when it closed. S says when pumpkin seeds are in the packet.
- Sunday bells: on the first Sunday of every month, each garden evening the church down the street rings its bells for evensong, in place of the evening tune. The first time they are named, and S mentions them.

Sound check plays the pumpkin's part and the Sunday bells.

## Challenges

Challenges are separate small gardens, the same for every player, each with a gentle goal and a twist announced before you start. Days pass the same way. If you choose Leave the challenge on the pause menu, your challenge garden waits for you until the challenge changes.

- Daily: 6 planters, 4 kinds of seed and 4 days to grow a three-part harmony. Each day has its own twist.
- Weekly: 9 planters and 8 days to harvest 5 tomatoes, with a twist for the week.
- Monthly: the rooftop festival. 12 planters, every seed, 4 sprouts already up, and 10 days to bring all 6 voices into one full chorus.

There are four twists. Heat wave: the soil dries much faster and no rain comes. Drizzly days: a shower every day, but slower growing. Aphid season: far more aphids. Green thumb: faster growing, and a little more thirst.

When the goal is met the garden celebrates and the challenge ends. If the days run out first, the garden closes for the season and your score still counts. Score: 5 for each harvest, 2 for each pest picked, 5 for each part of your fullest harmony, and 100 for meeting the goal, plus 10 for every day left over. Your best and your streak are read out at the end, and in the challenges menu. After the results, a short menu offers Play this challenge again, Hear the results again, and Back to the challenges. Escape does the same as Back to the challenges.

## Sound check

Sound check, on the title menu, plays each sound you need in the garden with its name and what it means: the sprout hum, dry soil, aphids, every tending sound, the rain, and each plant's part of the song on its own. Listen there whenever a sound is unfamiliar.

## Controls

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's spoken instructions follow your bindings.

- Arrow keys: step between planters. Up and Down change row, Left and Right change planter. Row 1 is the far row, ahead of you, and planter 1 is on the left.
- Space: water the plant under you. Watering soil that is already moist waterlogs it. On empty soil, Space opens your seed packet so you can plant something.
- Tab: tend the plant under you. It picks off aphids first, then harvests a ripe plant, then prunes leggy stems.
- Delete or Backspace: pull the plant up and add it to the compost, to make room for something else. You are asked first.
- Enter: sit on the bench, or get up again. While you sit, time passes 6 times faster, and you stand up as soon as a plant needs you.
- L: list every planter, row by row.
- S: status. What needs you, any rain, and the planter under you. Press it again straight away for the day, the time of day, the weather, the harmony and your harvests.
- R: repeat the last thing said.
- H: hint. Where the nearest job is and which key does it.
- Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title and Back to the lobby. During a challenge, Back to the title is Leave the challenge. Your garden is saved when you leave.
- F1: what the keys do on the screen you are on.
