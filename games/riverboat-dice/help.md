# Riverboat Dice

## About the game

It is a warm night on a paddle steamer, and you sit at the dice table with three other passengers. The night has 5 landings. Each landing, the dealer rolls two dice for everyone still seated, and the sums build up on the table. Stand up and bank what the table holds, or stay in for another roll. A 1 on either die ends the landing, and everyone still seated gets nothing for it. Whoever banks the most over the night wins. Nothing here is timed.

## A landing

Each landing starts with everyone seated and the table empty. Space calls a roll. The dealer shakes two dice in a cup, they land on the felt, and he calls the faces and the sum, such as "4 and 3, 7. The table holds 19." The sum of every roll in the landing adds up on the table. Everyone still seated stands to win what the table holds.

## Staying in and banking

After each roll, you choose. Space stays in for another roll. Tab stands you up and banks what the table holds into your night total, and a coin stack answers. Once you have banked, the table rolls on without you until the landing ends. Space or Tab while the dealer talks hurries him along. In your very first night, the first bank asks you to press Tab again to make sure.

## The 1

A 1 on either die ends the landing with a dull wooden thud and a groan from the table. The dealer says "A one. The landing is over." Everyone still seated gets nothing for the landing, but keeps what they banked in earlier landings. If everyone has banked before a 1 comes, the landing ends too. 11 of 36 rolls have a 1. Up tells you the odds at any time.

## The other passengers

Three passengers play against you, and each plays by one plain rule. The Widow sits on your left. She banks once the table holds 15. The Gambler sits across the table. He rides until it holds 40. The Captain sits on your right. He banks when he leads by 20, counting what everyone would have if they banked now, or after 4 rolls in a landing. A chair scrape from their seat tells you one has stood, and the dealer names them. None of them ever knows the next roll: they draw on the same dice you do.

Left and Right name each passenger in turn, with their night total, whether they are seated, and their rule. Down reads every night total, highest first.

## Hints

H says what a careful player would do now, and why. The careful player stays in while one more roll is worth more on average than what a 1 would take, and banks once the table holds 19 or more. With the snag rule on, your night total counts as at risk too. On the last landing, the careful player stays in while banking would not put them in the lead.

## Status and the last roll

S gives the landing, what the table holds and whether you are seated, always in that order. Press S again straight away for your night total and who is still in. R repeats the last roll and what it did: who stood, or who lost what when a 1 came.

## Scoring and records

Your score is your night total. Records keep your best night, how many nights you have won, and the longest stay: the most rolls anyone stayed seated through in one landing. A tie for the most shares the win.

## Challenges

Every player gets the same dice in the same order. The daily is one saloon night, new each day. The weekly is snag week: three saloon nights with the snag rule on, your three night totals added, new each Monday. With the snag rule, a double 1 is the snag: a crash of glass and wood, and it wipes the whole night total of everyone still seated. The monthly is the Captain's table: you and the Captain alone, for 10 landings. Your result counts when the last night ends. Leave the challenge, on the pause menu, does not count, so you can try again later.

## Calendar events

Showboat Saturday: on the second Saturday of every month, the Showboat Saturday row after Play opens. On other days it stays in its place, locked, and says when it opens. It is a saloon night of 5 landings against the same three passengers, with one change: doubles with no 1, from 2 and 2 up to 6 and 6, double what the table holds. A bright chime from the dealer's side tells you, and the dealer says "Showboat doubles. The table doubles to 24." The passengers keep their rules. Up gives the odds with the doubles in, and the careful player's hint stays in until the table holds 40. It is scored on a board of its own, and Records show your best. Once the day is over, Play again on the results says Showboat Saturday is over and takes you back to the title.

High water: from March to May, the spring river runs high. On saloon and full moon nights, a rushing current takes the paddle wheel's place under the table. The night's first words name it, and S and H mention it. The rules do not change, and challenges never hear it.

## Sounds

A leather cup rattles as the dealer shakes the dice, and the dice tumble onto the felt from the dealer's side, across the table. A 1 is a dull wooden thud and a groan. The snag is a crash of glass and wood. A chair scrape comes from the seat of a player who stands. A stack of coins is your own bank. The table cheers when you win the night. A dull tap means there is nothing to do just now. Under it all, the paddle wheel churns and the band plays in the saloon, kept quiet under speech. The sound check on the title plays each one with its name.

## Controls

Space: stay in, and call the next roll.

Tab: bank what the table holds and stand for the rest of the landing.

Left and Right: name each passenger, their night total and their rule.

Up: the odds.

Down: every night total, highest first.

S: status: the landing, the table and your seat. Press it again for your night total and who is still in.

R: the last roll and what it did.

H: what a careful player would do now, and why.

F1: help for what you are doing right now. F2 stops speech.

Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title is Leave the challenge.

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's spoken instructions follow your bindings.
