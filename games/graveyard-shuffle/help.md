## About the game

Doctor Volt's newest monster sat up on the slab and started to dance, and by midnight the whole graveyard wants to learn it. You lead the dance. The graveyard band plays, and each monster comes up with its own sound one beat before its move. Dance the move on the beat with that monster's key. Moves close to the beat score more. A song always plays to its end, and your score is what you danced.

## A Halloween special

Graveyard Shuffle turns up in the Seasonal games around Halloween: from two weeks before, October 17, to the day after, November 1.

Earn a star on all three songs of night 1 in that time and the game is yours to keep. After Halloween it moves to the Action games for the rest of the year. Until you keep it, it is gone from the lists after November 1, and your stars, records and challenge results wait for next Halloween.

Night 4, Doctor Volt's laboratory, is where the dance began, and it only opens around Halloween. The rest of the year, the laboratory and its three songs in the practice room wait for October 17.

## The monsters and their moves

The zombie's move is the drag. Dance it with the Down arrow.

The skeleton's move is the rattle. Dance it with the Up arrow.

The mummy's move is the unwrap. Dance it with Space.

The vampire's move is the swoop. Dance it with the Left arrow. The vampire comes on night 2.

The werewolf's move is the howl. Dance it with the Right arrow.

The ghost's move is the float. Dance it with Tab. The werewolf and the ghost come on night 3.

The first time each monster comes up in a dance, your screen reader names it and its key. A night that brings a new monster names it before the dance instead. After that, it is not named again, and F1 lists the moves. Before a dance starts, H plays every monster with its move, and each move key plays its own monster, so you can learn them with nothing at stake.

## The beat and timing

Each song starts with four count-in beats, then the music. A monster's sound comes one beat before its move, so listen for who comes up and dance on the next beat.

On the beat is spot on, worth 3 points. A little off is good, worth 2. Further off is close, worth 1. Early and late count the same. A missed or wrong move scores nothing.

The first time each of these happens in a dance, a single word names it.

## Streaks and stars

Every 8 moves in a row danced in time, you earn 4 bonus points. A miss or a wrong move starts the count again.

Each song earns stars from the share of its best points you earned: 1 star from half, 2 stars from three quarters, 3 stars from nine tenths. Streak bonuses do not count toward stars.

## The nights

There are four nights of three songs, each night a little faster than the one before, and each song played through twice.

Night 1, the graveyard gate: the zombie, the skeleton and the mummy, one monster a bar.

Night 2, the crypt: the vampire joins in, and now and then two monsters come in a row.

Night 3, the old church: the werewolf and the ghost join in, with more monsters between the beats.

Night 4, Doctor Volt's laboratory: every monster, and the fastest songs.

A star on every song of a night opens the next night, though night 4 opens only around Halloween. Play dances the first night you have not cleared. Night select dances any night you have opened.

## The songs

Night 1: The Graveyard Warm-up, Boneyard Boogie and The Shallow Grave Shuffle. Night 2: The Cobweb Crawl, The Coffin Lid Clap and Drip in the Crypt. Night 3: Bat Wing Boogie, The Full Moon Stomp and The Bell Tower Bop. Night 4: Lightning Rod Rock, Off the Slab and The Graveyard Shuffle.

## The practice room

The practice room plays one song of any night you have opened. When a move's beat comes, the song waits for you: the music holds, and the monster and its key are named. Dance the move and the song goes on. A wrong move names the right key. Nothing is scored.

## Challenges

Every player gets the same songs and monsters for each period.

Tonight's song, the daily: one song, with the zombie, the skeleton, the mummy and the vampire. A new one comes every day.

The weekly haunt: three songs, with every monster. A new one comes every Monday.

The long night, the monthly: five songs, with every monster and more of them. A new one comes on the first of every month.

Your score is every point and bonus you earn. Leave the challenge, on the pause menu, stops without counting the run, so you can try again later.

## Spoken call-outs

Each monster is a sound that plays with the music. With spoken call-outs on, your screen reader also names each monster as it comes up. It is off at the start. Switch it in Settings, from the title or the pause menu.

## Pausing

Escape pauses, and the music holds. When you come back, three beats count you back in, and the song goes on where it stopped.

## Controls

Down arrow: the zombie's drag.

Up arrow: the skeleton's rattle.

Space: the mummy's unwrap. Before a dance, Space starts it.

Left arrow: the vampire's swoop.

Right arrow: the werewolf's howl.

Tab: the ghost's float.

S: status: the song, the bar, your score and your streak.

R: play the last monster again and name it.

H: before a dance, play every monster with its move. In a dance, name the next monster, its key and how many beats away it is.

F1: help for what you are doing right now. F2 stops speech.

Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title is Leave the challenge.

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's spoken instructions follow your bindings.
