# Graveyard Shuffle

## About the game

Doctor Volt's newest monster sat up on the slab and started to shuffle, and by midnight the whole graveyard wants in. You lead the dance while the graveyard band plays: each monster steps up one beat ahead, and you hit that monster's key right on the beat to nail its signature move. Tighter timing scores higher, and every song plays all the way to the finish.

## A Halloween special

Graveyard Shuffle appears in the Seasonal games around Halloween, from October 17 to November 1, and stays two more weeks through November 15 so you have time to catch up.

Start a dance or a practice session during that window and the game is yours to keep, even if you leave early. Once the season ends, it moves into the Action games with all your progress and stars intact. If you never try it during the season, it leaves the list until next Halloween.

Night 4, Doctor Volt's laboratory, is where the dance began, and it only opens around Halloween. The rest of the year, the laboratory and its three songs in the practice room stay locked until October 17.

## The monsters and their moves

Six monsters join the floor across the four nights, each with a signature move.

- Zombie, the drag: press the Down arrow.
- Skeleton, the rattle: press the Up arrow.
- Mummy, the unwrap: press Space.
- Vampire, the swoop: press the Left arrow. The vampire joins on night 2.
- Werewolf, the howl: press the Right arrow. The werewolf joins on night 3.
- Ghost, the float: press Tab. The ghost also joins on night 3.

The first time each monster steps up in a dance, the game names it and its key, or introduces new monsters before a night begins. After that they stay out of the way, and you can press F1 any time for a reminder. Before a song starts, press H to preview every monster with its move, or press any move key to preview that monster on its own.

## The beat and timing

Every song opens with four count-in beats before the band kicks in. Each monster steps up one beat ahead, so catch who is up and hit their move on the very next beat.

Landing right on the beat is spot on for 3 points. Slightly off is good for 2 points, and a little further off is close for 1 point. Early and late count the same, while a missed or wrong move scores nothing.

The first time each judgment happens in a dance, a single spoken word names it for you.

## Streaks and stars

Every 8 moves you dance in time in a row earns a 4 point streak bonus. A miss or a wrong move resets your streak.

Each song awards stars based on the share of its maximum move points you earn: 1 star at half, 2 stars at three quarters, and 3 stars at nine tenths. Streak bonuses boost your score, though they do not count toward stars.

## The nights

The main game spans four nights of three songs, each night a little faster than the last, with every song playing through twice.

- Night 1, the graveyard gate: the zombie, the skeleton, and the mummy, one monster per bar.
- Night 2, the crypt: the vampire joins in, and two monsters occasionally step up back to back.
- Night 3, the old church: the werewolf and the ghost join in, with more moves between the beats.
- Night 4, Doctor Volt's laboratory: every monster on the floor, set to the fastest songs.

Earning at least 1 star on every song of a night unlocks the next night, though night 4 only opens around Halloween. Play jumps straight to the first night you have not cleared yet, while Night select lets you dance any night you have unlocked.

## The songs

- Night 1: The Graveyard Warm-up, Boneyard Boogie, and The Shallow Grave Shuffle.
- Night 2: The Cobweb Crawl, The Coffin Lid Clap, and Drip in the Crypt.
- Night 3: Bat Wing Boogie, The Full Moon Stomp, and The Bell Tower Bop.
- Night 4: Lightning Rod Rock, Off the Slab, and The Graveyard Shuffle.

## The practice room

The practice room lets you work through any song from a night you have unlocked at your own pace. Whenever a move's beat arrives, the music holds and names the monster and its key, then continues once you dance it. A wrong key reminds you of the right one, and nothing is scored.

## Challenges

Challenges give every player the exact same songs and monsters for each period.

- Daily, Tonight's song: a single song featuring the zombie, skeleton, mummy, and vampire, refreshed every day.
- Weekly, The weekly haunt: three songs featuring every monster, refreshed every Monday.
- Monthly, The long night: five busy songs featuring every monster, refreshed on the first of each month.

Your score totals every move and streak bonus you earn. Picking Leave the challenge on the pause menu exits without recording the run, so you can try again later.

## Spoken call-outs

Turn on Spoken call-outs in Settings from the title or the pause menu if you want the game to announce each monster's name aloud as it steps up. It starts off by default.

## Pausing

Press Escape any time to pause and hold the music. When you resume, three count-in beats bring you right back in where you left off.

## Controls

- Down arrow: the zombie's drag.
- Up arrow: the skeleton's rattle.
- Space: the mummy's unwrap, or start the dance before the music begins.
- Left arrow: the vampire's swoop.
- Right arrow: the werewolf's howl.
- Tab: the ghost's float.
- S: report the bar, score, and streak; press again for the song name and move breakdown.
- R: repeat and replay the last monster.
- H: before a dance, preview every monster with its move; during a dance, name the next monster, its key, and how many beats away it is.
- F1: open help for the current screen.
- F2: stop speech.
- Escape: pause the dance, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title becomes Leave the challenge.

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's instructions follow your bindings.
