# Little Rapids

## About the game

You sail a little boat down a stream. The water carries the boat along by itself, and you steer it from bank to bank to catch what floats down. Petals, leaves and paper scraps arrive on the beat of the music, and every catch plays a note in the river's song.

Reach the river mouth to earn stars. Catch more for more stars. Bumping a bank, a stone or a duck costs your combo, never the run.

## Steering and catching

Hold Left or Right to steer. The boat glides across the stream while you hold, and drifts to a stop when you let go. There are no lanes: the boat can be anywhere between the two banks.

Everything you hear floating toward you is placed where it really is. A sound to your left is to your left of the boat, and it gets louder and clearer as it comes closer. Steer so the sound is straight ahead of you as it reaches the boat, and you catch it.

A catch near the boat's very middle is a clean catch. It rings a little brighter and scores double. Settings has a Clean catch width row, Narrow, Normal or Wide, if clean catches feel too strict or too easy.

## What floats down

The first time you meet each thing on the water, play stops and a page names it. Enter carries on.

- Petal: a soft bell. The common catch, 10 points.
- Leaf: a low wooden note. Also 10 points.
- Paper scrap: a papery flutter. 10 points, and scraps fold into new boats in the Boatyard.
- Trinket: a bright sparkle, and rare. 100 points, and it goes on your keepsake shelf.
- Stone: a low gurgle. Steer clear of it.
- Gate: chiming water between two stones. Go through the middle for 40 points. Going round costs nothing.

## The current

The current is a soft rushing hiss that wanders across the stream. While the boat rides in it you score 2 points every beat, and a gentle swish tells you when you slip in. A duller tone tells you when you slip out. H says which way the current is.

## Combo and the song

Each catch in a row adds 1 to your combo. Every 10 in a row raises your multiplier by one, up to times 5, with a rising chime. The multiplier counts on every point you score.

The river's song grows with your combo. A bass line plays from the start, then plucked strings, a pad, a second tune and soft drums join as the combo climbs. A bump costs the combo and the band thins out again, but the song never stops.

## Bumps

The banks lap softly on both sides, and the nearer bank grows louder as you drift toward it. Touch a bank and the boat bumps with a wooden knock. Stones, mill wheels and ducks bump you too, each with its own sound.

A bump sets your combo back to nothing and shakes the boat, so it steers slowly for a moment. The leaf boat keeps half its combo. Outside the endless river, a bump never ends the run.

## The streams

Play, on the title, sails the stream you sailed last, or the newest one you have opened. Choose a stream, just below it, lists all four with their stars and bests. Stars from one open the next.

- Garden brook: a gentle brook with a few stones and gates. Open from the start.
- Rain gutter: waterfalls to steer through. Opens at 2 stars.
- Mill race: mill wheels that block one side. Opens at 4 stars.
- Market canal: ducks that paddle across your path. Opens at 7 stars.

Rain gutter waterfalls roar through a gap in the rocks. Steer into the loudest part of the rush for 40 points, or you bump the rocks. A mill wheel creaks and thumps on one side of the stream, heard early: be on the other side as it passes. A duck quacks and paddles across, so its sound moves: steer clear of it.

## Stars and ribbons

Reach the river mouth for 1 star. Catch 6 in every 10 things offered for 2 stars, and nearly 9 in 10 for 3 stars. The mouth sounds with a run of bells, and the results follow.

Each stream has 3 ribbons: catch a number of things in one run, make a number of clean catches in one run, and one of its own, such as going through every waterfall in the Rain gutter. A ribbon counts only when you reach the mouth, and each one gives 10 paper scraps. In Choose a stream, F1 on a stream reads its ribbons.

## Boats

You start with the paper boat. Paper scraps fold more in the Boatyard, on the title.

- Paper boat: steady. The boat you start with.
- Newspaper boat: wide, so it catches more, but it turns slowly. 25 scraps.
- Leaf boat: light and quick, and a bump costs only half your combo. 50 scraps.

In the Boatyard, Enter folds a boat you have scraps for, or sails one you already own.

## The keepsake shelf

Trinkets are lost things the streams carry: buttons, keys, a thimble and more. Each stream has its own, and you find ones you have not caught before first. The Keepsake shelf, on the title, lists each one with a line about it.

Some trinkets belong to a set. Find a whole set and the shelf tells its little story. The music box set plays its tune.

## The endless river

The endless river opens with your first star on Garden brook. It is made of every stream you have opened, and gets busier the further you go. Your score is how far you sail, in metres.

On the endless river each bump makes the boat soggier, and the count is said. The fifth bump sinks it. Every 20 catches dry the boat out by one bump.

## Drift

Drift is slow water with the whole band playing from the start. There is no score, no stars, and the banks only nudge you. Sail it to listen and unwind.

## Calendar events

Two days a month change your own streams and the endless river. Challenges never see them.

- Duck parade, the second Monday of every month: more ducks paddle across every stream, and each one you pass is worth 20 points.
- Lantern float, the third Friday of every month: lanterns float down every stream. A lantern rings with a glassy chime and is worth 50 points.

## Challenges

Challenges are streams that are the same for every player.

- Today's stream: a stream with waterfalls, mill wheels and ducks, on the paper boat. New every day.
- Boat of the week: a longer stream of mill wheels and ducks, on a boat chosen for the week.
- The long run: the longest stream, with everything, and it gets busier all the way to the mouth. New every month.

Your best and your streak are read at the end, and in the challenges menu.

## Settings

Settings, on the title or the pause menu, has the game's own rows after speech, volume and controls.

- Clean catch width: Narrow, Normal or Wide.
- Gentle streams: fewer things float down and the current swings less, on every stream and the endless river. Challenges are not changed.
- Say trinket names: says a trinket's name as you catch it. Off, the results name them.
- Banks louder up close: the nearer bank grows louder as you drift toward it. Off, both banks stay soft.

## Sound check

Sound check, on the title, plays everything you need to know on the water, each with its name: every floating thing as it floats up to you, with the sound it makes as it reaches you, the banks, the current, a clean catch, a bump on the bank, and the multiplier going up.

## Controls

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's spoken instructions follow your bindings.

- Left and Right arrows: hold to steer the boat across the stream.
- Space: before the stream starts, starts it at once.
- S: your combo and multiplier, and a bank if one is close. Press it again straight away for your score, how far down the stream you are, your best, and the current.
- H: where the current is, and what is coming next.
- R: repeat the last thing said.
- Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title and Back to the lobby. During a challenge, Back to the title is Leave the challenge.
- F1: what the keys do on the screen you are on.
