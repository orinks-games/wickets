# Little Rapids

## About the game

Launch a paper boat onto a winding stream and let the water carry you downstream. Steer from bank to bank to scoop up petals, leaves, paper scraps, and rare trinkets on the beat, building a combo that adds new instruments to the river's song.

Reach the river mouth to earn stars, fold new boats from your scraps, and fill your keepsake shelf. On the four main streams, bumping a bank, a stone, or a duck resets your combo, but it never ends your run.

## Steering and catching

Hold Left or Right to paddle across the stream, and let go to drift to a stop. Left Shift and Right Shift give you one hard stroke in that direction when you need a quick dodge. There are no lanes, so your boat can glide anywhere between the two banks.

Everything floating toward you approaches from its real spot across the stream. Steer until an item is lined up straight ahead of your boat as it arrives, and you will scoop it right up.

Catching something near the dead centre of your boat counts as a clean catch and scores double points. If clean catches feel too strict or too generous, you can adjust Clean catch width in Settings between Narrow, Normal, and Wide.

## What floats down

The first time you meet a new object on the water, play pauses briefly to introduce it, and Enter sends you back on your way.

- Petal: the everyday catch, worth 10 points.
- Leaf: worth 10 points.
- Paper scrap: worth 10 points, and used to fold new boats in the Boatyard.
- Trinket: a rare catch worth 100 points that goes onto your keepsake shelf.
- Stone: a hazard in the water. Steer clear so you do not bump it.
- Gate: a gap between two stones. Thread the middle for 40 points, or go round safely for no penalty.

## The current

The main current winds back and forth across the stream. Riding inside it earns you 2 points on every beat. Press H at any time to check which way the current lies.

## Combo and the song

Each catch in a row adds 1 to your combo, and every 10 catches raises your multiplier by 1, up to times 5. Your multiplier applies to every point you score.

As your combo climbs, more instruments join the river's song. A bump resets your combo and thins the band back out, though the song keeps flowing.

## Bumps

Drifting into a bank bumps your boat, and so do stones, mill wheels, and ducks.

A bump resets your combo to 0 and shakes the boat so it steers slowly for a moment. The leaf boat softens the blow by keeping half its combo. Outside the endless river, a bump never ends your run.

## The streams

Play on the title launches the stream you sailed last, or the newest one you have opened. Choose a stream lists all four waters with your stars and best scores, and earning stars opens the next stream downstream.

- Garden brook: a gentle brook with a few stones and gates. Open from the start.
- Rain gutter: fast water with waterfalls to steer through. Opens at 2 stars.
- Mill race: turning mill wheels that block one side of the stream. Opens at 4 stars.
- Market canal: lively ducks paddling across your path. Opens at 7 stars.

Rain gutter waterfalls pour through gaps in the rocks, so steer through the opening for 40 points instead of bumping the stone. Mill wheels block one half of the stream, so tuck onto the clear side as they pass. Ducks paddle right across the water, so steer around them.

## Stars and ribbons

Reaching the river mouth always earns you 1 star. Catch at least 60 percent of the items offered for 2 stars, or at least 85 percent for 3 stars, and your results follow as soon as you reach the mouth.

Each stream also has 3 ribbons: one for catching a set number of items in a run, one for clean catches, and one special ribbon such as threading every waterfall in Rain gutter. Ribbons count only when you finish at the mouth, and each one awards 10 paper scraps. Press F1 on a stream in Choose a stream to check its ribbons.

## Boats

You begin with the paper boat, and you can spend paper scraps in the Boatyard on the title to fold new ones.

- Paper boat: steady all-round handling. Yours from the start.
- Newspaper boat: wider so it catches more easily, though it turns more slowly. Costs 25 scraps.
- Leaf boat: light and quick to steer, and a bump costs only half your combo. Costs 50 scraps.

In the Boatyard, Enter folds a boat once you have enough scraps, or switches to one you already own.

## The keepsake shelf

The streams carry lost trinkets like buttons, keys, and a silver thimble. Each stream has its own collection, and you will always find ones you are missing before repeats. Open the Keepsake shelf from the title to browse every trinket you have fished out and read a short note about each.

Some trinkets belong to a matching set. Complete a whole set and the shelf tells its story, and the finished music box plays its tune.

## The endless river

The endless river opens as soon as you earn your first star on Garden brook. It stitches together every stream you have unlocked and grows busier the farther you travel, scoring your run in metres.

On the endless river, every bump makes your boat soggier, and the fifth bump sinks it. Catching 20 items dries the boat out by one bump.

## Drift

Drift puts you on slow, easy water with the whole band playing from the start. There is no score and there are no stars, and the banks only nudge you gently back into the stream.

## Calendar events

Two monthly events visit your regular streams and the endless river, while challenges stay unchanged.

- Duck parade, on the second Monday of every month: extra ducks paddle across every stream, and each one you pass safely is worth 20 points.
- Lantern float, on the third Friday of every month: glowing lanterns drift down every stream, worth 50 points each.

## Challenges

Challenges give every player the exact same stream to sail.

- Today's stream: a daily run with waterfalls, mill wheels, and ducks in the paper boat.
- Boat of the week: a longer weekly stream of mill wheels and ducks in a chosen boat.
- The long run: a monthly endurance stream with every hazard, growing busier all the way to the mouth.

Your best score and streak appear at the end of each run and inside the Challenges menu.

## Settings

Open Settings from the title or the pause menu to find Little Rapids options right below speech, volume, and controls.

- Clean catch width: choose Narrow, Normal, or Wide for how close to centre a clean catch must be.
- Gentle streams: sends fewer items down the water and calms the current on every stream and the endless river. Challenges are not changed.
- Say trinket names: announces a trinket's name as you catch it. Turn it off to wait for the results instead.
- Banks louder up close: raises the nearer bank as you drift toward it. Turn it off to keep both banks soft.

## Sound check

Open Sound check from the title to preview every floating item, obstacle, and cue on the water. Select any entry to play it as it arrives at your boat.

## Controls

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's instructions follow your bindings.

- Left and Right arrows: hold to paddle across the stream.
- Left Shift and Right Shift: take one hard stroke that way. These two keys stay the same even if you change your bindings.
- Space: launch the boat right away before the stream begins.
- S: check your combo, multiplier, and any nearby bank. Press S again right away for your score, progress down the stream, best score, and current.
- H: check where the current is and what is coming up next.
- R: repeat the last spoken line.
- Escape: pause the run for Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, or Back to the lobby. During a challenge, Back to the title becomes Leave the challenge.
- F1: check what the keys do on the current screen.
