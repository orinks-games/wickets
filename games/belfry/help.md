# Belfry

## About the game

The old bell ringer left a secret sequence of 4 bells, chosen from 6. This sequence is called a peal. Work out the bells and their order within 10 guesses.

Each guess is a row of bells. Ring your row and listen to the apprentice's knocks and taps for clues. If you use all 10 guesses, the secret sequence is read out. Nothing here is timed.

## The bells

There are 6 bells, numbered 1 to 6. Bell 1, the treble, is the highest. Bell 6, the tenor, is the lowest. Whenever you pick a bell, it rings and its number and name are spoken, such as "Bell 3, middle high".

## Building a row

Your row has 4 places: Left and Right move, and each move rings and names that place. Up and Down change the bell, wrapping from 6 to 1. Tab plays your whole row back without ringing it for an answer, so it uses no row.

## Ringing a row and the answer

Space rings your row. That uses one of your rows. After the bells, the apprentice answers. The answer counts bells that are in the right place and bells that belong elsewhere in the peal, such as "2 in place, 1 misplaced". The answer never says which bells it means. Solve the peal by placing every bell correctly: four bells, or five in the cathedral.

Each bell of your row counts once at most. If the peal is 1, 1, 2, 2 and you ring 1, 2, 1, 1, the first bell is in place, and one more 1 and one 2 are misplaced: 1 in place, 2 misplaced.

A row rings out over a second or two. Any key but Escape while it rings skips straight to the spoken answer.

## Village and Plain

Village is the main game. A bell may come twice in the peal, or more, such as 3, 1, 3, 5. Plain never puts a bell in the peal twice, which makes it a gentler start. Both give you 10 rows.

## The row log

R plays the last answer's knocks and taps again, then reads every row so far with its answer, such as "Row 2: 1, 1, 4, 5. 2 in place, 1 misplaced." S gives the status: the row you are on, your place, the row you are building and the last answer. S again gives rows left and hints.

## Hints

H names one bell, and says whether it is in the peal. A hint is always true, and never names the same bell twice. Each hint uses one of your rows, and halves your score. A hint needs 2 rows left, so your last row is always kept for ringing.

## Scoring and records

Solving a peal scores 100, plus 100 for each row left. Each hint halves it. Records keep your best score, the fewest rows you solved a peal in, and your streak of peals solved in a row, for Village and Plain apart. A peal that runs out of rows ends the streak, and so does a peal you leave after ringing a row.

## Challenges

Every player gets the same challenges. The daily is one Village peal, new each day. The weekly is five Village peals in a row, with their scores added, new each Monday. The monthly is the cathedral peal: 5 places, 8 bells to choose from, 12 rows and no hints. Bells 7 and 8 ring below the village tenor, and bell 8 is the cathedral's tenor. Your result counts when the last peal ends. Leave the challenge, on the pause menu, does not count, so you can try again later.

## Events

Two rows join the title on their nights only. Each keeps its own best score, fewest rows and streak, apart from Village and Plain, and Records reads them once you have played them.

Practice night is on the second Wednesday of every month. The peal has 5 places from 6 bells and 10 rows. The tower captain calls one place: as the peal begins, the place and its bell are named, and that bell is already set in your row. Its cue plays whenever you move onto that place. S names it too. The other places you find as usual.

## Sound check

Sound check on the title plays the cues you need during play.

## Controls

Left and Right: move between the places of your row.

Up and Down: change the bell in your place.

Space: ring your row for an answer.

Tab: play your row back without ringing it for an answer.

S: status: the row you are on, your place, your row and the last answer. Again: rows left and hints.

R: the last answer, then every row so far.

H: a hint, which uses a row.

F1: help for what you are doing right now. F2 stops speech.

Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title is Leave the challenge.

Number keys, on the briefing before a peal: play the sounds the peal needs. Enter or Space starts it.

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's spoken instructions follow your bindings.
