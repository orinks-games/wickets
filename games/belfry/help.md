# Belfry

## About the game

The old bell ringer left a secret sequence of 4 bells, called a peal, chosen from 6. Work out which bells are in the peal and put them in the right order within 10 tries.

Each try is a row of bells. Ring your row, and the apprentice answers with clues about how close you are. If all 10 rows run out, the secret peal is revealed. Nothing here is timed.

## The bells

The tower has 6 bells, numbered 1 to 6. Bell 1 is the treble, the highest bell, and bell 6 is the tenor, the lowest. Whenever you pick a bell, it rings and you get its name, such as "Bell 3, middle high".

## Building a row

Your row has 4 places. Left and Right move between them, ringing and naming each place as you land on it. Up and Down change the bell in your current place, wrapping from 6 back to 1. Tab plays your whole row back without submitting it for an answer, so it does not use a row.

## Ringing a row and the answer

Space rings your row for an answer, using one of your rows. After the bells finish, the apprentice gives your clue: how many bells are in the right place, and how many belong somewhere else in the peal, such as "2 in place, 1 misplaced". The clue never says which specific bells it means. Solve the peal by getting every bell into place: 4 bells normally, or 5 in the cathedral.

Each bell in your row counts at most once. For example, if the peal is 1, 1, 2, 2 and you ring 1, 2, 1, 1, the first bell is in place, and one extra 1 and one 2 are misplaced: 1 in place, 2 misplaced.

A row takes a couple of seconds to ring out. Pressing any key except Escape skips straight to the answer.

## Village and Plain

Village is the standard game, where the same bell can appear more than once in the peal, such as 3, 1, 3, 5. Plain never repeats a bell, which makes it a friendlier place to start. Both modes give you 10 rows.

## The row log

R repeats the last answer, then reads back every row you have rung so far with its result, such as "Row 2: 1, 1, 4, 5. 2 in place, 1 misplaced." S reads your status: row number, current place, the row you are building, and the last answer. S again reads your rows left and hints used.

## Hints

H reveals whether one bell is in the peal or not. Hints are always true and never repeat a bell. Taking a hint costs one of your rows and halves your score, and you need at least 2 rows left so your final row is always saved for ringing.

## Scoring and records

Solving a peal earns 100 points, plus 100 more for every row you have left. Each hint halves your score. Records track your best score, fewest rows, and solve streak separately for Village and Plain. Running out of rows ends your streak, and so does leaving a peal after you have rung a row.

## Challenges

Every player gets the same challenges. The daily challenge is one Village peal, fresh each day. The weekly challenge is 5 Village peals in a row with their scores added together, fresh each Monday. The monthly challenge is the cathedral peal: 5 places from 8 bells, 12 rows, and no hints. Bells 7 and 8 sit below the village tenor, making bell 8 the cathedral tenor.

Your challenge result counts once the last peal ends. Picking Leave the challenge from the pause menu does not count the run, so you can try again later.

## Events

Special event rows join the title on their nights only. Each event keeps its own best score, fewest rows, and streak apart from Village and Plain, and Records lists them once you have played them.

Practice night runs on the second Wednesday of every month. You get 5 places from 6 bells and 10 rows, and the tower captain calls one place for you. As the peal starts, that place and its bell are named and already set in your row. Its cue plays whenever you move onto that place, and S names it too. Work out the remaining places as usual.

## Sound check

Sound check on the title plays each bell and cue you need in the tower.

## Controls

Left and Right: move between the places in your row.

Up and Down: change the bell in your current place.

Space: ring your row for an answer.

Tab: play your row back without ringing it for an answer.

S: status, including row number, place, your row, and the last answer. Press S again for rows left and hints.

R: repeat the last answer, then read every row rung so far.

H: take a hint, which uses one row.

F1: help for what you are doing right now. F2 stops speech.

Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title becomes Leave the challenge.

Number keys, on the briefing before a peal: play the sounds the peal needs. Enter or Space starts it.

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's instructions follow your bindings.
