# Dustfall

## About the game

You hold a dome on a dusty world through twelve nights of raiders. Each night they come over the horizon in waves and fall down five lanes toward the dome. You have a gun on a rail along the dome's edge, a few missiles, and the lanes you mined. Each raider is heard where it is in the sky, and it comes closer as it falls.

Between nights the colony phase opens. You spend the salvage the raiders leave on mines, missiles and the buildings that keep the colony fed, powered, shielded and firing. Hold all twelve nights and Relief lands. After that the long watch opens, where the nights go on until the dome falls.

## The lanes

The sky has five lanes: far left, left, centre, right and far right. A raider arrives at the horizon, far out, and falls through mid range and near range to the dome. A raider that reaches the dome breaches it: the shield takes the hit first, then the dome. The dome starts at 100 and the colony falls at 0.

Everything you hear is placed where it is. A raider on your left is heard from a lane to your left, and it grows louder and nearer as it falls. The gun is heard under the lane it stands in, and while a raider is in the gun's lane a lock tone pulses under it, faster as the raider nears.

## The night

Left and Right slide the gun a lane. Space fires up the gun's lane at the nearest raider in it for 1 damage, and the gun needs a moment to reload between shots, twice as long when the colony has no food. Tab fires a missile at the nearest raider in any lane for 4 damage, enough for anything but a mothership or the carrier.

The raiders come in waves. The forecast says how many. A wave is called, its raiders come over the horizon one after another, and when the last of them is down the next wave is called after a short lull. The night is held when the last wave is down.

Scanning costs nothing. Up and Down step through the raiders, nearest the dome first, naming each with its lane, its range and the hits it has left. S gives the gun, the dome, the shield and the wave, and a second press reads every raider. H reads the sky lane by lane and says what comes next.

## Raiders

- Drifter: 1 hit. From mid range it fires a bolt down the gun's lane. You hear a rising whine in that lane, and the bolt lands unless the gun has left the lane by then.
- Skitter: 1 hit, and it comes in pairs. It falls fast.
- Hulk: 3 hits. It falls slowly, and from near range it siphons the shield, which stops the shield recharging for a while.
- Sapper: 2 hits. It takes the lane over your hydroponics, or over another building, and darkens the building it breaches over.
- Mule: 2 hits. It crosses the sky at mid range from one edge to the other and never attacks. Shoot it before it leaves for its food and metal.
- Mothership: 8 hits. It comes on nights 5 and 10, holds at mid range, slides from lane to lane, drops drifters, and winds up siege shots that cannot be dodged. Its hull turns the gun away while it is far out: let it come to mid range, or spend a missile.
- Carrier: 12 hits, on night 12 with two motherships. It crosses the sky at mid range, letting skitters down as it goes, and fires siege shots.

## Quirks

From night 7 a raider or two may come down with a quirk. The scan names it with the raider, and the forecast names it when a scanner works or once Quirks in the forecast is unlocked.

- Armoured: takes 1 hit more than its kind.
- Hasty: falls faster than its kind.
- Weaving: slips to the next lane, away from the gun, as it reaches mid range.

## The colony phase

The colony phase opens before each night with your stores: power, metal and food. The colony eats 2 food a night. With none, the gun reloads at half speed.

- Ready for the night starts the night. The forecast on its row says what is coming and in how many waves.
- Repair the dome puts 20 back for 5 metal.
- Make a missile costs 6 power and 4 metal, or half with a workshop. The rack holds 3.
- Build puts a building in an open slot under the left, centre or right lane.
- Lay a mine puts a mine in a lane for 3 metal, one a night. It goes off under the first raider past mid range in that lane, for 2 damage, and is spent.
- The colony reads the stores, the missiles, what stands in each slot and where the mines lie.

The game saves at the start of each colony phase. Leaving during a night brings you back to that colony phase, with what you had bought.

## Orders

Each colony phase offers two orders, and you may take one. Each is a gain now for a cost in the night to come.

- Overclock: 20 shield now, up to the shield's cap. After the night the colony pays 4 power.
- Rationing: 4 food now. Tonight's waves come closer together, and so do the raiders in them.
- Salvage: every kill tonight drops twice the metal. The skitters fall half as fast again.

## Buildings

- Solar array: 8 metal. Makes 3 power a night.
- Hydroponics: 6 metal and 2 power. Grows 2 food a night. Sappers go for it.
- Scanner: 8 metal and 2 power, and 1 power a night. The forecast names the lanes the raiders enter at, and H names the lane the next raider takes.
- Workshop: 10 metal. Halves the cost of a missile.
- Shield coil: 8 metal, and 2 power a night. Adds 10 shield a night, and the shield recharges twice as fast in the night.
- Flak post: 6 metal and 1 power, and 1 power a night. Through the night it fires up its own lane every few seconds at the nearest raider short of the horizon, for 1 damage. It scatters off a mothership or the carrier.

A breach that reaches the dome darkens the building under that lane, as does any sapper's breach. A dark building does nothing until repaired for 4 metal. A building that needs power and gets none that night does nothing either.

## The long watch

Once a colony of yours has held to Relief, The long watch opens on the title. It is a colony that goes on past Relief: night 13 brings night 9's raiders round again with another drifter, and each round of four nights adds one more, until the dome falls. Its record is the most nights held.

## Dust storm

On the fourth Monday of every month, Dust storm opens on the title: a colony through twelve nights with a lane of dust drifting across the sky. The dust moves a lane at a time, far left to far right and back, and its wind is heard where it is. Raiders in the dust cannot be scanned or heard and fall on regardless, and the flak post under it is silent. The storm's best score is its own record. On other days the row says when the storm next comes.

## Tech and unlocks

Holding a night earns 1 tech, a mothership down earns 3, and Relief earns 5. Tech adds up across every colony, and Unlocks, on the title, lists what it opens: a thicker dome, a bigger shield, more slots, a missile at the start, two mines a night, a deeper rack, quirks in the forecast and the lanes in the forecast without a scanner. The modes open on what one colony did: The long watch when Relief has landed, and Hard vacuum, which caps the shield at 30 for 25 percent more score, once a colony has held 8 nights. Reva's logbook opens a page for each night you have held at most. Playing a whole colony with the call-outs off earns 25 percent more score too.

## Challenges

Challenges, on the title, gives everyone the same sky. Tonight's sky is one night, the raiders of a night between 3 and 9, on a colony already built for them: the gun is yours from the first wave. Siege week is five nights, 4 to 8, on a colony holding the stores the first nights would have brought, so spend them before the raiders come. The long watch is a whole colony, to Relief and on past it until the dome falls. Only your best result on each is kept, and a run left from the pause menu does not count. Challenges play without your unlocks, so the sky is the same for everyone.

## Scores and records

Each raider down scores points, 10 for a drifter or skitter up to 300 for the carrier. Holding a night scores 50, and a night with no breach scores 50 more. When Relief lands, the power and metal in store are added. Records keeps your best score, the most nights you have held, your longest watch, your best in the dust storm and your tech in all.

## Settings

Settings, on the title or the pause menu, has the game's own rows after speech, volume and controls.

- Spoken call-outs: names each raider with its lane as it arrives, comes near and fires. Off, you hear the engines and the shots, and only a breach is named.
- Scan order: nearest first, or left to right across the sky.
- Storm wind: whether the dust storm's wind is heard at the lane it covers.

## Sound check

Sound check, on the title, plays every cue you need to know, each from where you would hear it.

## Controls

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's spoken instructions follow your bindings.

- Left and Right arrows: slide the gun a lane. Hold to keep sliding.
- Space: fire up the gun's lane.
- Tab: fire a missile at the nearest raider anywhere.
- Up and Down arrows: scan the raiders.
- S: the gun, the dome, the shield and the wave. Press it again straight away for every raider.
- H: the sky lane by lane, and what comes next.
- R: repeat the last thing said.
- Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title and Back to the lobby.
- F1: what the keys do on the screen you are on.
