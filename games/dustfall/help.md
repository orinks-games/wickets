# Dustfall

## About the game

You hold a dome on a dusty world through twelve nights of raiders. Each night they come over the horizon onto a grid of five columns and six rows and walk down toward the dome. You have a gun on a rail along the dome's edge, a few missiles, and two actions a turn. When your turn ends the raiders move, and each one is heard at its new place on the grid.

Between nights the colony phase opens. You spend the salvage the raiders leave on walls, flak posts and mines for the grid, and on buildings that keep the colony fed, powered and shielded. Hold all twelve nights and Relief lands.

## The grid

The grid has five columns, far left, left, centre, right and far right, and six rows. Row 6 is the horizon, where raiders arrive. Row 1 is at the dome. A raider on row 1 breaches on its next move.

Everything you hear is placed where it is. A raider's step on your left comes from a column to your left, and it grows closer as it comes down the rows. The gun is heard under the column it stands in.

## Your turn

Each turn you have 2 actions, or 1 when the colony has no food. Sliding the gun one column, firing, or firing a missile each costs an action. Ending the turn with actions left puts 5 shield on the dome for each one, up to 50.

The gun hits the nearest raider in its own column for 1 damage. A missile hits the nearest raider anywhere on the grid for 4 damage, enough for anything but a mothership or the carrier.

Scanning costs nothing. Up and Down step through the raiders, nearest the dome first, naming each with its column, its row and the hits it has left. H says where the scanned raider is heading. S gives the gun, the dome, the shield and your actions, and a second press reads every raider.

## Raiders

- Drifter: 1 hit. From 3 rows out it fires a bolt at the gun's column. You hear a rising whine under that column, and the bolt lands when the turn ends unless the gun has moved off it. Drifters keep out of flak cover.
- Skitter: 1 hit, and it comes in pairs. It takes 2 steps a phase and slips to the side the gun is not on.
- Hulk: 3 hits. It rests every other phase, bashes through walls when the way round is long, and from 2 rows out it siphons the shield, which stops it charging that turn.
- Sapper: 2 hits. It heads for your hydroponics and darkens the building under the column where it breaches.
- Mule: 2 hits. It crosses row 4 from one edge to the other and never attacks. Shoot it before it leaves for its food and metal.
- Mothership: 8 hits. It comes on nights 5 and 10, holds at the centre of row 3, drops skitters, and winds up siege shots that cannot be dodged.
- Carrier: 12 hits, on night 12 with two motherships. It crosses back and forth on row 4, firing siege shots.

A raider that reaches row 1 breaches the dome's edge on its next move. The shield takes the hit first, then the dome. The dome starts at 100 and the colony falls at 0.

## Routes

Raiders find their own way. Each one looks for the shortest path to its goal and goes round walls, and a drifter also goes round flak cover unless the way round is too long. A hulk bashes a wall when that is shorter than going round, and any raider walled in bashes its way out. The same grid always gives the same routes, so what you build changes where they walk, and H tells you where.

## The colony phase

The colony phase opens before each night with your stores: power, metal and food. The colony eats 2 food a night. With none, you get 1 action a turn.

- Ready for the night starts the night. The forecast on its row says what is coming.
- Repair the dome puts 20 back for 5 metal.
- Make a missile costs 6 power and 4 metal, or half with a workshop. The rack holds 3.
- Build puts a building in an open slot under the left, centre or right column.
- Place a wall, a flak post or a mine on rows 2 to 5. Left and Right choose the column, Up and Down the row, and Enter places it.
- The grid and The colony read what stands where.

The game saves at the start of each colony phase. Leaving during a night brings you back to that colony phase, with what you had bought.

## Works on the grid

- Wall: 3 metal. Blocks a sector. Two bashes bring it down.
- Flak post: 6 metal, and 1 power a night. Each phase it fires once at the nearest raider in the sectors beside it. Drifters keep out of its cover. It scatters off a mothership or the carrier, and without power it does nothing.
- Mine: 2 metal. Goes off under the first raider to step on it, for 2 damage.

## Buildings

- Solar array: 8 metal. Makes 3 power a night.
- Hydroponics: 6 metal and 2 power. Grows 2 food a night. Sappers go for it.
- Scanner: 8 metal and 2 power, and 1 power a night. The forecast names the columns the raiders enter at, and H gives a raider's whole route and the phase it lands.
- Workshop: 10 metal. Halves the cost of a missile.
- Shield coil: 8 metal, and 2 power a night. Adds 10 shield a night.

A breach that reaches the dome darkens the building under that column, as does any sapper's breach. A dark building does nothing until repaired for 4 metal.

## Challenges

Challenges, on the title, gives everyone the same sky. Tonight's sky is one night, the raiders of a night between 3 and 9, on a colony already built for them: the gun is yours from the first turn. Siege week is five nights, 4 to 8, on a colony holding the stores the first nights would have brought, so spend them before the raiders come. The long watch is a whole colony, twelve nights to Relief. Only your best result on each is kept, and a run left from the pause menu does not count.

## Scores and records

Each raider down scores points, 10 for a drifter or skitter up to 300 for the carrier. Holding a night scores 50, and a night with no breach scores 50 more. When Relief lands, the power and metal in store are added. Records keeps your best score and the most nights you have held.

## Settings

Settings, on the title or the pause menu, has the game's own rows after speech, volume and controls.

- Spoken call-outs: names each raider's step with its column and row. Off, you hear the steps and only a raider reaching the dome is named.
- Confirm end of turn: Enter asks before ending a turn, and a second Enter ends it.
- Scan order: nearest first, or left to right across the grid.

## Sound check

Sound check, on the title, plays every cue you need to know: each raider's step, the gun, a charge, a breach, the works on the grid and the turn chimes. Each is played from where you would hear it.

## Controls

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's spoken instructions follow your bindings.

- Left and Right arrows: slide the gun one column. One action.
- Space: fire at the nearest raider in the gun's column. One action.
- Tab: fire a missile at the nearest raider anywhere. One action.
- Enter: end the turn. Actions left charge the shield.
- Up and Down arrows: scan the raiders.
- S: the gun, the dome, the shield and your actions. Press it again straight away for every raider.
- H: where the scanned raider is heading.
- R: repeat the last thing said.
- Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title and Back to the lobby.
- F1: what the keys do on the screen you are on.
