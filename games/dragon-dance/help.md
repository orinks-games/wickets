# Dragon Dance

## About the game

Lead the dragon through the Lunar New Year parade. Keep your head in the pearl's lane on every beat, rear up on the gong, catch red envelopes, and avoid firecrackers. Each street plays to the end and awards up to 3 stars.

## The street and its lanes

A street has 3, 4 or 5 lanes, numbered from the left. Left and Right move your head one lane at a time, and each move says the lane you are in. At the edge of the street your head stays where it is. Whatever is in your lane is straight ahead.

## Following the pearl

The pearl's cue comes from its lane: to your left, in front, or to your right. When it moves, its cue moves with it. It only moves on the first beat of a bar, and that beat is free: you have until the next beat to reach it. The arrival cue confirms that your head has reached the pearl's lane. Tab rings the pearl's chime again and names its lane. With spoken call-outs on, each move is also named, such as "Pearl, lane 3."

## Beats, points and combos

The drum marks every beat and the start of each bar. On every beat that your head is in the pearl's lane, the dragon dances and scores 1 point for each segment of its body. Beats on the pearl in a row build your combo. Every 16 beats in a row earn 50 bonus points. One beat away from the pearl, to catch an envelope or dodge, keeps the combo. Two beats away in a row break it.

## Gongs and rearing up

Drum roll: gong in 2 beats. Press Space right on the gong to rear for a perfect, 100 points; a little off is good, 60. Missing a gong scores nothing. Rearing when no gong is near breaks your combo, so wait for the roll.

## Red envelopes

A red envelope approaches down a lane. It is heard far ahead and comes closer over 4 beats. Be in its lane on the beat it reaches you to catch it: 40 points, and the dragon grows one segment longer, so every beat after scores 1 more. Many envelopes come down the pearl's own lane. Others come down the lane beside it: step over for that beat, then back. Up names what is coming and how many beats away it is.

## Firecrackers

Firecrackers approach down a lane over 4 beats. Keep your head out of that lane on the beat they reach you. If your head is in it, the tail stumbles over them, and you lose 50 points and your combo. Firecrackers never end the dance, and they are never in the pearl's lane: stay with the pearl and you are safe. On the later streets they wait in the lane the pearl has just left, so follow quickly.

## Shops and crowds

Shops line both sides of the street, one every other bar. As you pass a shop, its crowd claps politely, or cheers if your combo is 4 or more, for 20 points. The cheer comes from the side the shop is on. With spoken call-outs on, a cheering shop is named, such as "The tea house cheers, on your left."

## Stars and the streets

The parade has 8 streets, from Market Row to The Grand Avenue. Each is faster and wider than the one before: Market Row has 3 lanes and no firecrackers, The Grand Avenue has 5 lanes and quick drums. At the end of a street you earn stars by your share of the best score the street allows: 35 percent for 1 star, 60 percent for 2, and 80 percent for 3. 1 star opens the next street. Play takes you to the next street without a star. Street select lists every street with your stars, and locked streets say what opens them.

## Practice street

Practice street is short and slow, with every call spoken. Stumbles cost nothing, and nothing is saved.

## Lunar New Year on the real calendar

The parade follows the real calendar. The year's animal follows the lunar year, which turns on Lunar New Year, so in January and early February it is still last year's animal. In the 10 days before Lunar New Year, lanterns go up over the shops and red envelopes count double. Lunar New Year dates are known from 2024 to 2040. After 2040 the festival days do not open, and the New Year parade and Lantern walk rows say so.

## The New Year parade

On Lunar New Year, and the day either side of it for time zones, the New Year parade opens on the title. On other days its row is locked and says how many days are left. The year's animal greets you with a line as the parade begins. It is a long street of 4 lanes with many red envelopes. It ends with a countdown from 5 to midnight, then the great gong: rear up on it for double points.

## Keepsakes

Dancing the New Year parade to the end earns a keepsake for the year's animal, such as a brass ox bell for the Year of the Ox. There are 12 animals, one each year, so collecting them all takes twelve New Years. Keepsakes, on the title, lists the ones you hold, the year you earned each, and the animals still to come, then the lanterns you have found.

## The Lantern walk

The Lantern Festival is the fifteenth day of the new year, 14 days after Lunar New Year. On that day, and the day either side, the Lantern walk opens on the title. It is a calm walk at a slow drum, on a quiet evening street, with no firecrackers and no gongs. Lanterns come down the lanes instead of envelopes, catch them as you would an envelope. There are 12 kinds of lantern, such as the rabbit lantern and the carp lantern, and each new one joins your collection.

## Challenges

Every player gets the same challenges. The daily is one street of 4 lanes and 24 bars, new each day. The weekly is the long parade, 48 bars with a gong every other bar, new each Monday. The monthly is the grand festival: 5 lanes, quick drums and firecrackers close behind the pearl, new each month. Your result counts when the street ends. Leave the challenge, on the pause menu, does not count, so you can try again later.

## Spoken call-outs

Spoken call-outs, a row in Settings from the title or the pause menu, switches the spoken calls in play. On, a voice names the pearl's lane when it moves, each red envelope, lantern and firecracker string with its lane as it starts to come, and each cheering shop. Off, none of these are spoken. Practice street always speaks its calls. Status, Up and the hint work either way.

## Status, what is coming and hints

S gives your lane, the pearl, everything coming with its lane and beats, and the next gong. Press S again straight away for your score, the dragon's length, your combo and the bar. Up names only what is coming. Down describes your dragon: its length, envelopes caught and combos. R repeats the last call. H gives a hint for this moment, such as which way the pearl is or what to do about firecrackers in your lane.

## Sound check

Sound check on the title plays the cues you need during play.

## Controls

Left and Right: move your head one lane.

Space: rear up, as the gong strikes.

Tab: name the pearl's lane.

Up: what is coming down the lanes, and the next gong.

Down: your dragon's length and combo.

S: your lane, the pearl, what is coming and the next gong. Press it again for score, length, combo and bar.

R: repeat the last call.

H: a hint for this moment.

F1: help for what you are doing right now. F2 stops speech.

Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title is Leave the challenge.

{beta} Number keys, on the briefing before a street: play the sounds the street needs. Enter or Space starts it.

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's spoken instructions follow your bindings.
