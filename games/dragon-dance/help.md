# Dragon Dance

## About the game

Step into the head of a paper dragon and lead it down a bustling Lunar New Year parade. Weave across the street to stay on the pearl every beat, rear up when the gong strikes, scoop up red envelopes to grow longer, and steer clear of firecrackers. Every street plays all the way to the finish and awards up to 3 stars.

## The street and its lanes

Each street has 3, 4, or 5 lanes, numbered from 1 on the far left. Left and Right move the dragon's head one lane at a time, announcing the lane you step into. At either edge of the street your head stays put, and anything in your own lane sits straight ahead.

## Following the pearl

The pearl travels in one lane at a time: to your left, straight ahead, or to your right. It only changes lanes on the first beat of a bar, and that beat is free, giving you until the next beat to catch up.

Once your head reaches the pearl's lane, an arrival cue confirms you are on it. Press Tab any time to check the pearl's lane again. With Spoken call-outs on, every move is also announced, such as "Pearl, lane 3."

## Beats, points and combos

The drum keeps every beat and marks the start of each bar. On every beat your head spends in the pearl's lane, the dragon dances and scores 1 point for each segment of its body.

Consecutive beats on the pearl build your combo, earning 50 bonus points for every 16 beats in a row. Ducking away for a single beat to grab an envelope or dodge a hazard keeps your combo alive, while two beats away in a row break it.

## Gongs and rearing up

When the drum roll starts, a gong is 2 beats away. Press Space right on the gong to rear up for a perfect 100 points, or close to it for a good 60 points. Missing a gong scores nothing, and rearing when no gong is coming breaks your combo, so wait for the roll.

## Red envelopes

Red envelopes travel down a lane toward you, closing in over 4 beats. Be in the envelope's lane on the beat it reaches you to catch it for 40 points and add a new segment to the dragon, raising every beat's score by 1 point from then on.

Many envelopes come straight down the pearl's lane, while others travel in a lane beside it so you can step over for one beat and step right back. Press Up any time to check what is coming and how many beats away it is.

## Firecrackers

Firecrackers approach down a lane over 4 beats, so keep your head clear of that lane when they arrive. If you stay in their path, the tail stumbles, costing you 50 points and your combo.

Firecrackers never end the dance, and they never appear in the pearl's lane, so sticking with the pearl keeps you safe. On later streets they often land in the lane the pearl just left, so follow swiftly.

## Shops and crowds

Shops line both sides of the street, one every other bar. As you pass a shop, its crowd claps politely, or bursts into cheers for 20 points if your combo is 4 or higher. With Spoken call-outs on, cheering shops are announced, such as "The tea house cheers, on your left."

## Stars and the streets

The main parade runs across 8 streets, from Market Row to The Grand Avenue, growing wider and faster as you go. Market Row starts gentle with 3 lanes and no firecrackers, while The Grand Avenue brings 5 lanes and quick drums.

At the end of a street, you earn stars based on your share of the street's top score: 35 percent for 1 star, 60 percent for 2 stars, and 80 percent for 3 stars. Earning 1 star unlocks the next street. Play jumps straight to your next unstarred street, while Street select lets you pick any street and see what unlocks locked ones.

## Practice street

Practice street is short and slow, with every call spoken aloud. Stumbles cost nothing and no score is saved, so you can warm up without pressure.

## Lunar New Year on the real calendar

Dragon Dance follows the real calendar. The year's zodiac animal turns on Lunar New Year, so in January and early February it is still the previous year's animal.

During the 10 days leading up to Lunar New Year, lanterns go up over the shops and red envelopes score double. Lunar New Year dates are built in from 2024 to 2040; after 2040, the festival days no longer open, and the New Year parade and Lantern walk rows explain why.

## The New Year parade

On Lunar New Year, plus the day before and the day after for time zones, the New Year parade opens on the title screen. On other days it stays locked and counts down the days remaining.

The year's animal greets you as the parade begins. You dance down a long 4-lane street packed with red envelopes, ending in a countdown from 5 to midnight and the great gong, where rearing up scores double points.

## Keepsakes

Finishing the New Year parade earns a keepsake for that year's zodiac animal, such as a brass ox bell for the Year of the Ox. With 12 animals in the cycle, collecting the full set spans twelve New Years. Open Keepsakes from the title to check the keepsakes you hold, the year you earned each, the animals still ahead, and the lanterns you have collected.

## The Lantern walk

The Lantern Festival falls on the fifteenth day of the new year, 14 days after Lunar New Year. On that day, plus the day either side, the Lantern walk opens on the title screen.

It is a relaxed evening walk to a slow drum with no firecrackers and no gongs, where lanterns drift down the lanes in place of red envelopes. Catch them just like envelopes to collect all 12 kinds, from the rabbit lantern to the carp lantern.

## Challenges

Challenges give every player the exact same street for each period.

- Daily, Today's street: 4 lanes and 24 bars, refreshed every day.
- Weekly, The long parade: 48 bars with a gong every other bar, refreshed every Monday.
- Monthly, The grand festival: 5 lanes with quick drums and firecrackers close behind the pearl, refreshed each month.

Your score is recorded when the street finishes. Picking Leave the challenge on the pause menu exits without counting, so you can try again later.

## Spoken call-outs

Toggle Spoken call-outs in Settings from the title or the pause menu. When on, a voice announces the pearl's lane as it moves, each red envelope, lantern, and firecracker string as it starts approaching, and each cheering shop. When off, those stay silent in regular play, though Practice street always keeps them on, and Status, Up, and Hint work either way.

## Status, what is coming and hints

Press S for your lane, the pearl's lane, everything approaching with its lane and beats, and the next gong; press S again right away for your score, dragon length, combo, and bar. Press Up to check only what is coming, Down to check your dragon's length, envelopes caught, and combos, R to repeat the last call, and H for a hint on what to do right now.

## Sound check

Open Sound check on the title screen to preview every gameplay cue you need on the parade route.

## Controls

- Left and Right arrows: move the dragon's head one lane.
- Space: rear up as the gong strikes.
- Tab: check the pearl's lane.
- Up arrow: report what is coming down the lanes and the next gong.
- Down arrow: report your dragon's length and combo.
- S: report your lane, the pearl, upcoming items, and the next gong; press again for score, length, combo, and bar.
- R: repeat the last call.
- H: get a hint for right now.
- F1: open help for the current screen.
- F2: stop speech.
- Escape: pause the parade, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title becomes Leave the challenge.
- Number keys: on the briefing before a street, preview the cues for that street; Enter or Space starts the parade.

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's instructions follow your bindings.
