## About the game

The alley has closed for the night, and lane nine is all yours for ten frames of bowling. Pick your stance, angle your aim into the pocket, and time your release at the top of the swing to chase strikes, pick up spares and push for a perfect 300. Every standing pin is always named by number after each roll.

## Standing and aiming

The lane is 39 boards wide, with board 1 at the left gutter, board 39 at the right, and board 20 lined up straight with the head pin. Use Left and Right arrows to step one board at a time along the approach.

Press Up arrow to turn your aim one step right, or Down arrow to turn one step left. Each step shifts where a clean ball crosses the pins by one board, marked by the aim cue and named aloud.

## The pocket

Stand off centre and angle your shot into the pocket beside the head pin, around board 17 or board 23. Rolling straight into the head pin often leaves a tough split like the 4, 6, 7 and 10.

Coming in at an angle, such as standing on board 12 aiming 5 right or board 28 aiming 5 left, carries the pins much better. Even a clean pocket ball does not strike every single time, since the lane and pins bring a touch of chance to every roll.

## The swing and the release

Press Space to start your swing, then press Space again right at the top of the swing to release the ball. A clean release follows your aim straight down the lane.

Releasing early or late pulls the ball off line, with a bigger pull the further you are from the top, and the lane determines which way it pulls. If you hold on through the whole swing, the ball releases late at the end.

## The ball and standing pins

A gutter ball knocks down no pins. After every roll, the standing pins are named by number, and the ball-return cue marks when you are ready to roll again.

Pin 1 is the head pin, followed by 2 and 3, then 4 through 6, and the back row 7 through 10 with 7 on the left corner and 10 on the right. A split is any leave where the head pin is down and a gap separates the remaining pins.

## Scoring

Knock down all ten pins on your first ball of a frame for a strike, worth 10 plus your next two balls. Clear all ten across both balls for a spare, worth 10 plus your next ball, while any open frame scores the pins you knocked down.

In the tenth frame, a strike earns two bonus balls and a spare earns one. String together twelve strikes in a row for a perfect 300.

## Hints

Press H for a suggested stance and aim tailored to the pins still standing. On a full rack it recommends an angled pocket line, and on a spare it favors a forgiving line that still works if the ball drifts a board, while estimating how many pins a clean release should take.

## Pumpkin night

From 17 October to 1 November, Pumpkin night opens on the title, while the rest of the year it shows as locked with its opening date. It plays a full ten-frame game with pumpkins for pins, standard scoring, numbered leaves and its own best score.

## Records

Open Records to review your high game, longest strike streak, high series from the monthly challenge, whether you have bowled a perfect game, and your total strikes and spares.

## Challenges

Every player bowls on the same lane for each challenge period, so an early or late release pulls the same way for everyone.

Tonight's lane, the daily: one full game, refreshing every day.

Spare week, the weekly: 20 leaves with one ball at each, scored by how many spares you pick up, refreshing every Monday.

The late league, the monthly: a three-game series scored by your combined total, refreshing on the first of each month.

Choosing Leave the challenge from the pause menu ends the run without counting it, so you can come back and try again later.

## Pausing

Press Escape to pause at any time. If you pause mid-swing, the swing resets without releasing the ball so you can start fresh when you resume.

## Controls

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's instructions follow your bindings.

Left arrow: step one board left.

Right arrow: step one board right.

Up arrow: turn your aim one step right.

Down arrow: turn your aim one step left.

Space: start the swing, and press again to release the ball.

Tab: not used. There is no spin, so every ball rolls straight along your aim.

S: status, naming the frame and ball, standing pins, your board and your aim. Press S again right away for the score.

R: repeat the last result.

H: suggest a board and aim for the standing pins.

F1: help for the current screen. F2 stops speech.

Escape: pause to open Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, or Back to the lobby. During a challenge, Back to the title becomes Leave the challenge.
