# Liberty Lights

## About the game

Step up as the director of a Fourth of July fireworks show. Each level plays a pattern of fireworks across the night sky, and your job is to launch the same show back in the right spots and right on beat. Score 60 or more out of 100 to clear a level and work through twenty shows to unlock the endless grand finale, or head into Freestyle to compose and save shows of your own.

From the title menu, Play opens your next uncleared level, Level select lets you jump to any of the twenty, and Grand finale opens once you clear level 20. You will also find Freestyle, Challenges, Independence night on the Fourth of July, Practice room, Tutorial, Help, Sound check, Records and Settings.

## A round

1. Press Space to start the show. It begins with three count-in beats half a second apart, followed by the go cue that marks time zero.
2. The fireworks launch across the sky. Two seconds after the last one, the game tells you it is your turn.
3. Press Space again for your own count-in, matching the show's pace, and launch each firework at the same moment after the go cue.
4. Press Enter when you finish launching. The crowd reacts, and the game reads your score.

Once the show has played, pressing Tab replays it from the start and clears anything you have launched so far.

## Choosing with Up and Down

While launching, Space triggers whatever choice you have selected, and Up and Down cycle through your options: boom, crackle, whistle, sparkle, take back the last firework and finish the show. It starts on boom, and pressing a number key launches that firework and switches Space to it as well. On a controller, A launches, Up and Down on the d-pad pick your option, and holding Left or Right aims. On a keyboard, Enter still finishes and Backspace still takes back your last launch.

## Fireworks

- 1: launch a boom.
- 2: launch a crackle.
- 3: launch a whistle.
- 4: launch a sparkle.

The number keys on the keypad work the same way.

## Left, center and right

Starting at level 11, fireworks launch on the left and right as well as the center. Hold the Left arrow or A while launching to fire on the left, or hold Right or D to fire on the right; with neither held, the firework goes straight up the center. Pressing Left or Right on its own plays the aim cue on that side so you can check your aim.

## Scoring

Each firework in the show is paired with whichever of your launches matches it best:

- 50 points for the right kind of firework.
- 25 points for the right position.
- Up to 25 points for timing: 25 within a tenth of a second, 20 within 0.3 seconds, 15 within half a second, 10 within a second and 5 within two seconds.

Every extra or missing firework deducts 20 points. Your final mark is out of 100, and 60 or more clears the level.

## Combos and show points

Matching a firework with the right kind, right position and timing within 0.3 seconds starts a combo, and each consecutive match builds it higher. Every step adds bonus points: 50 for the second in a row, 100 for the third, and so on. Combo bonuses count only when the show clears at 60 or more.

Show points equal your score times ten, plus any combo bonuses, and every level saves its best show points. Your run total adds up every level cleared in a row from where you started and tracks a best score too. Replaying a level resets your run total to where it stood before that level, so each level counts once. Saving a new freestyle show clears the best score of the show it replaces.

Asking for a hint takes 15 points off your score for that show.

## Levels

- Levels 1 to 5: one firework, matching its kind and timing.
- Levels 6 to 10: two fireworks.
- Levels 11 to 15: three fireworks across the left, center and right.
- Levels 16 to 20: four to eight fireworks, with some launching early and overlapping the one before.

After level 1, a show can begin up to two seconds after the go cue, so match when the first firework goes up as well as what it is.

## Challenges

Challenges on the title, or from the lobby, offer three shared events that are identical for every player: a new daily every day, a weekly every Monday and a monthly on the first of each month. Each row shows the challenge, your best result so far and your streak. Selecting one opens a short briefing with the twist and how long until the next reset; press Enter on the last page to begin or Escape to back out. A challenge counts once its final show ends, while leaving via Leave the challenge or Back to the challenges between shows does not count it. Your score is the total show points across all shows in the event.

- Daily, tonight's show: one show from levels 11 to 20 under a twist.
- Weekly, the five-show gauntlet: five shows in a row, each larger than the last, under the week's twist. Scoring under 60 percent ends the gauntlet, though your points still count.
- Monthly, the festival: a themed festival, such as Independence Day in July, with two acts and a 14-firework finale where every act plays regardless of score. Each festival features a signature firework, and on any cleared show each signature firework matched within 0.3 seconds in the right spot adds 100 bonus points.

The twists are:

- Quickstep: shows run at double speed, with fireworks a second apart instead of two.
- Tough crowd: timing points apply only within 0.3 seconds, with nothing awarded beyond that.
- One shot: each show plays once, with replays and hints turned off.
- Night fog: the show is muffled by fog, though firework positions and your own launches are unchanged. You can sample the fog boom in Sound check.

## Results

After clearing a level, the results menu offers Next level, Play again, Hear the results again, Compare and Back to the title. Play again generates a fresh show for the level, Hear the results again reads your score and firework-by-firework breakdown, and Compare plays the original show followed by your own launches. If you do not clear, Try again starts a new show while Practice this level replays the exact same one. Escape returns to the title.

After a challenge show, the menu ends with Back to the challenges, and between shows in a weekly or monthly event the top option continues to the next show.

## Freestyle

Design your own fireworks show using 1 to 4 and aiming with Left and Right, with no clock and no score to worry about. The show's timeline begins on your first launch. Backspace removes the last firework and Enter finishes. Once done, you can play your show for the crowd, save it or choose Play again for a fresh canvas. Saved shows can be replayed from the freestyle menu or played back with Match your saved show, scored just like a campaign level.

## Grand finale

Clearing level 20 unlocks the grand finale, an endless streak of shows that grow longer and faster with fireworks across the whole sky. The run continues until a round scores under 60, and your highest finale total is saved as a record.

## Independence night

On the Fourth of July, Independence night opens on the title with a special holiday show. It stays open from the third to the fifth of July so players in every time zone can catch it on the Fourth. The rest of the year the row is locked, and pressing F1 tells you how many days remain.

The night runs through four acts, and every act plays regardless of score:

1. The opening salute: six booms alternating left and right, then two in the center, the same tradition every year.
2. Stars and stripes: sparkles and crackles alternating across the sky.
3. Whistles over the river: ten fireworks, mostly whistles, with some launching early.
4. The grand finale: sixteen rapid fireworks of every kind, capped by a closing salute of four booms on left, center, right and center.

The boom is the night's signature firework. On any act that clears, every boom matched in the right spot within 0.3 seconds adds 100 bonus points.

During the week before the Fourth, pressing Enter on the row plays a rehearsal of the opening salute so you can get a head start.

Finishing all four acts saves that year's keepsake with your best show points and most acts cleared, which you can revisit in Records.

## Practice room

The practice room is an unscored launch range where you can get comfortable with the controls. Launch with 1 to 4 or Space, aim with Left and Right, and each firework is named after it goes up. Escape pauses.

## Sound check

Sound check plays the cues used to match a show. On any firework row, press Enter to play it in the center, or Left and Right to play it on that side.

## Controller

On a controller, A is the main action: it plays the show, starts your count-in and launches your chosen firework. Up and Down on the d-pad choose a firework, take back or finish, and holding Left or Right aims to that side. X replays the show, Y reports status, the left bumper gives a hint, clicking the right stick repeats instructions, Back reads help and B or Start pauses.

## Controls

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's instructions follow your bindings.

- Space: start the show, begin your count-in or trigger the launch choice selected with Up and Down.
- 1 to 4: launch a boom, crackle, whistle or sparkle.
- Hold Left or A, Right or D: aim your launch to that side.
- Enter: finish your show and score it.
- Backspace: take back your last firework. Enter and Backspace cannot be changed in Settings.
- Tab: replay the show from the start.
- R: repeat the instructions for the current step.
- S: check the level, firework count, how many you have launched and the time since the go cue. Press S again straight away for the level style, any twist and your points so far.
- H: describe the show as a hint, at the cost of 15 points.
- F1: context help for the current screen.
- Up and Down: choose what Space does while launching, whether a firework, take back or finish.
- Escape: pause the game for help, settings or returning to the title or challenges.
