# Cupid's Quiver

## About the game

Climb onto the rooftops at dusk with Cupid's bow and shoot down paper hearts drifting across the evening sky. Turn to track each target, draw the string, lead the drift, and match hearts of the same colour back to back for sweetheart pair bonuses. Each rooftop gives you a quiver of arrows and a target count to clear, and falling short ends the evening.

## Aiming

Left and Right turn the bow 5 degrees per press, up to 70 degrees in either direction. The sky turns with you, so a heart directly in front of your bow sits straight ahead, while hearts off to either side pull left or right, and farther targets sit deeper in the distance.

Press Up any time to face straight ahead again. Each step of the bow announces your current aim, such as "15 degrees right", unless Spoken aim is turned off in Settings.

## Drawing and shooting

Hold Space to draw the bow. It reaches full draw after nearly a second, and releasing after the full-draw cue looses your arrow. If you let go too early, you keep the arrow instead. Press Down while drawing to ease the string back without shooting.

## Leading a heart

Hearts keep drifting while your arrow is in flight. A near heart takes a quarter of a second to reach, while a far one takes nearly three quarters of a second, so distant targets need more lead.

Aim a little ahead of a heart in the direction it is drifting. Your sights cue marks the moment a shot released now will meet the target. Press H any time to get the best target and the exact angle to meet it.

## Sweetheart pairs

Hearts drift across the sky in pairs of matching colour: red, pink, peach, violet, or white. The second heart of a pair arrives a moment after the first from either side.

Hit both hearts of a pair one after the other to score a sweetheart pair for 50 bonus points. A miss, or hitting a different target in between, breaks the pair. S, Tab, and H always name every heart's colour.

## Scoring

A near heart scores 10 points, a middle heart scores 20, and a far heart scores 30. Each consecutive hit adds 5 extra points, climbing up to 25 bonus points on a streak.

Sweetheart pairs add 50 bonus points, roses add 25 on top of their distance score, golden hearts are worth 100 points, and love notes score 40.

## The rooftops and moonlight

The main game climbs across six rooftops, from the bakery all the way to the cathedral. Each round gives you a quiver of arrows and a goal of hearts to hit before your quiver runs dry. Later rooftops hand you more arrows, more targets at once, faster drift, farther hearts, and a tighter aim.

Clear all six rooftops in a single evening to unlock Moonlight, where endless rounds play out under the moon and grow faster each time until you fall short.

## Love notes

Every so often, a folded love note drifts slowly across the rooftops. Shoot it down to read and keep the note, even if you leave the evening early. There are 24 notes to collect in all, and you can read every one you have found from Love notes on the title screen.

## Valentine's Day

Cupid's Quiver follows the real calendar. During the 7 days before the 14th of February, roses drift among the hearts for 25 extra points each.

On Valentine's Day itself, plus the day before and the day after so every time zone is covered, two special modes unlock. Valentine's night sends you across four moonlit rooftops with fast golden hearts worth 100 points. For two plays the same night with two players sharing one bow, taking turns one arrow at a time for a shared score.

Playing either mode saves a keepsake for that year in the Keepsake box on the title screen. The rest of the year both rows stay locked, and their help counts down the days until Valentine's Day returns.

## The practice field

The practice field gives you endless arrows and one slow heart at a time, never at far range, with nothing saved. It is the place to get comfortable turning the bow, drawing, and leading a moving target. Press Escape whenever you want to pause or leave.

## Challenges

Challenges give every player the exact same sky for each period.

- Daily, Today's sky: one long round of 15 arrows with a daily twist, such as a breeze that speeds up hearts, a far sky, a crowded sky, or double-value sweetheart pairs.
- Weekly: three rooftops back to back, each busier than the last, refreshed every Monday.
- Monthly: five rounds under the full moon with roses in the sky.

In weekly and monthly challenges, every round plays out and your points add up across all of them. Your result is recorded when the final round ends, while picking Leave the challenge from the pause menu exits without counting so you can try again later.

## Spoken call-outs

Two toggles in Settings control spoken call-outs, available from the title or the pause menu during a run. Spoken aim announces your angle each time you turn the bow, such as "15 degrees right"; turn it off and S still reports your aim whenever you ask.

Spoken targets names each target as it enters the sky, such as "Pink heart, from the left"; turn it off and Tab still lists everything in the sky. The first time you meet a new target or cue, the game explains it once, while anything already covered by the tutorial stays out of your way.

## Sound check

Open Sound check on the title screen to preview every gameplay cue you need on the rooftops.

## Controls

- Left and Right arrows: turn the bow 5 degrees.
- Up arrow: face straight ahead.
- Down arrow: ease the bowstring without shooting, or count your remaining arrows.
- Space: hold to draw and release at full draw to shoot; between rooftops, climb to the next one.
- Enter: climb to the next rooftop between rounds.
- Tab: list everything in the sky from left to right.
- S: report whose turn it is in For two, your aim, draw progress, arrows left, and hearts hit and needed; press again right away for the round, score, and target count.
- R: repeat the last announcement.
- H: get a hint naming the best target and how far to turn to meet it.
- F1: open help for the current screen.
- F2: stop speech.
- Escape: pause the game, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title becomes Leave the challenge.

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's instructions follow your bindings.
