## About the game

Fly a survey lander down to a pad on a far moon. Gravity pulls; hold the engine to push back. Touch down under safe speed, or you crash. Fuel is short: fall freely first, burn near the pad.

This help gives heights and speeds in metric. Set Units to US in Settings and the game says feet and feet a second instead, with call-out marks every 300, 150 or 30 feet.

## Starting a descent

Each flight starts with the lander waiting high above the pad. You hear the moon, its gravity, the height and the safe speed. Space starts the descent. Let go of Space after starting: the engine fires on the next press.

## The engine and fuel

Hold Space to fire the main engine, and let go to fall. Firing slows your fall and burns fuel, measured in seconds of burn. The game warns you at 25 percent and 10 percent fuel. When the tank is empty, the engine stops.

## Safe speed

Keep your falling speed below the safe speed before touching down. The game says Too fast when you cross above the limit, and Safe speed when you return below it. Near the pad, hold the engine until you hear Safe speed.

## The pad beacon

The pad beacon helps you judge how close you are to the ground. Sound check, on the title, lets you hear the beacon and the other gameplay cues.

## Call-outs

Mission control calls your height as you pass each mark, with your falling speed in metres a second. The marks are every 50 metres to start. The Up arrow calls less often and the Down arrow more often: every 100, 50 or 10 metres. The game remembers your choice, except on the practice pad. Tab asks for a call-out now, with your height, speed and fuel. R repeats the last call-out.

## Status and hints

S gives your height, your falling speed in metres a second, and your fuel. Press S again straight away for the moon, its safe speed and a hint. The pad is always straight below. H says what to do now, such as fall for about 10 seconds, then burn, or burn now and hold for about 3 seconds. The hint never asks for a burn longer than the fuel you have.

## The moons

The survey has three moons, opened one at a time. Land on a moon to open the next.

Moon 1, Thistle: light gravity, 300 metres up, safe speed 3 metres a second.

Moon 2, Marrow: medium gravity, 450 metres up, safe speed 3 metres a second. You start falling faster.

Moon 3, Ember: heavy gravity, 600 metres up, safe speed 2.5 metres a second, and a smaller tank.

Moon select flies any moon you have opened.

## Pace

The lander holds still while a call-out or another line is still being spoken. While you hold the engine and it burns, the lander keeps flying through speech. This holds at every pace and in every mode.

Pace in Settings sets how fast the descent runs: relaxed, normal or quick. Normal is the default and a little slower than quick. Quick keeps the original timings, and relaxed runs at half speed. Your fall, the engine, the fuel and the flight time follow the pace.

A change applies from your next flight. A challenge and the practice pad keep the pace they started at until you leave them.

Relaxed scores and the softest relaxed touchdown are saved separately, including challenges and events. Normal and quick share their scores. Moons you open count at every pace.

## Scoring and records

A landing scores 200 points. Fuel left adds up to 300 points, for a full tank. A soft touchdown adds up to 500 points: the slower you touch down, the more it pays. A crash scores nothing.

Records keeps your best score on each moon, your softest touchdown, how many landings and crashes you have made, and your challenge results.

## The tutorial

The tutorial is four short lessons on the practice pad, with endless fuel. Lesson 1, the fall: start the descent and hear the tone climb. Lesson 2, the engine: hold it until Safe speed. Lesson 3, the hint: ask when to burn. Lesson 4, the landing: the tutorial says Burn now when it is time, then Let go once you are slow. After a crash it says what to change, and you try again from the top.

## The practice pad

The practice pad flies the first moon with endless fuel, and call-outs every 10 metres once you fall slowly enough to hear them. Nothing is saved but a count of practice landings. After each touchdown you go back to the top for another try.

## Challenges

Every player gets the same moons for each period. A crash ends a run, and the landings before it still count.

Today's landing, the daily: one moon made from the day's seed, with a tank twice the best burn. A new one comes every day.

The weekly survey, the weekly: three moons in a row on one tank of fuel. What you burn on the first is gone for the next. The fuel bonus is paid once, at the end. A new one comes every Monday.

The deep moon, the monthly: one heavy moon with little spare fuel, a safe speed of 2.5 metres a second, and no hints. A new one comes on the first of every month.

Leave the challenge, on the pause menu, stops without counting the run, so you can try again later.

## Special nights

- Full moon pad, on every full moon night. The title has a row for it. It flies one moon made from that full moon's date, so every player gets the same moon that night, and it is named for the full moon, such as the Harvest Moon or the Hunter's Moon. Its tank holds twice the best burn.
- Tranquility pad, from 19 to 21 July, around the day of the first moon landing. The title has a row for it. You land at Tranquility Base in light gravity, 400 metres up, with a roomy tank. Land safely and you hear that the Eagle has landed.

Each keeps its own best score. Neither opens survey moons or sets your softest touchdown. Once its row has closed, Play again says so and takes you back to the title.

## Pausing

Escape pauses, and the lander stops in place until you resume. When you come back you hear your height and speed. A key you held into the pause does not fire the engine until you let it go and press it again.

## Controls

Space: before the descent, start it. In flight, hold to fire the main engine.

Up arrow: call the altitude less often.

Down arrow: call the altitude more often.

Left arrow and Right arrow: not used. This lander has no side thrusters.

Tab: ask mission control for a call-out now.

S: height, speed and fuel. Press it again for the moon, the safe speed and a hint.

R: repeat the last call-out.

H: a hint on what to do now. Not on the monthly challenge.

F1: help for what you are doing right now. F2 stops speech.

Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title is Leave the challenge.

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's spoken instructions follow your bindings.
