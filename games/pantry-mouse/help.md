## About the game

You are a little mouse slipping through a dark farmhouse pantry after midnight, hunting down every last crumb while the house cats prowl the aisles. Clear a pantry maze to move on to the next one, where the cats grow quicker. The ginger cat stalks you from pantry 1, and the tabby joins the chase in pantry 2.

Getting caught costs a whisker, and losing all 3 ends the run.

## Moving

Use the arrow keys to move one cell up, down, left, or right, or hold an arrow to scurry along at a steady pace. Step onto a crumb to eat it. If you bump into a wall, the game names the open ways out of your cell, such as "Open: up and left."

The cats stay asleep until you take your first step in each pantry.

## Finding crumbs

Press Space to sniff around. It marks the 3 nearest crumbs with directional cues, closest first, and tells you how many crumbs are left in the pantry.

Press H to get the direction and distance to the nearest crumb with no cat nearby, such as "Up, 3 steps." If the game says "No safe crumb", every remaining crumb has a cat close by. Hints are turned off in the monthly challenge.

## The cats

Each cat gives off a position cue once it is within 5 steps of you. The first time you meet a cat in a run, the game names it and its direction. Press F1 during play for a quick reminder of how each cat hunts.

The ginger cat always takes the shortest path straight to you.

The tabby cat wanders at random until it gets within 4 steps, then locks on and chases like the ginger cat.

The grey cat wanders just like the tabby cat and appears only in the monthly challenge.

A cat-close warning means a cat is within 2 steps. The first 3 times it triggers in a run, it also names the cat and its direction, such as "Ginger cat close, right." That direction points toward the cat, so head another way.

## Freezing

Press Tab to freeze in place for a moment. The nearest cat loses your scent until just after the freeze wears off, though other cats keep hunting and any cat that walks straight onto your cell still catches you. A thaw cue marks when you can move again. You get 3 freezes per pantry.

## Whiskers and catches

Getting caught costs a whisker and sends you and the cats back to your starting spots. The cats wait 2 seconds before moving again, and "Cats moving" marks when the chase resumes. In the practice pantry, getting caught costs nothing.

## Status

Press S for your status in the same order every time: where each cat is relative to you in cells, such as "ginger cat 3 cells left and 1 up", followed by crumbs, treats, and whiskers left. Press S a second time right away for your remaining freezes, score, and current pantry.

## Pace

The cats and your freeze timer pause while a line is being spoken. Pressing an arrow during speech moves you right away, and holding an arrow steps on as soon as speech finishes. Asking for the same line over and over stops holding the game after a while. This works the same way at every pace and in every mode.

Pace in Settings sets how quickly the cats move: relaxed, normal, or quick. Normal is the default and sits just below quick. Quick is the fastest, while relaxed runs at half speed. The cats, your freezes, and the pauses between pantries all follow your chosen pace.

Pace changes apply on your next run, and any run or challenge keeps its starting pace until it ends.

Relaxed scores are tracked on their own boards, including challenges and special nights, while normal and quick share their scores. Your deepest pantry counts at every pace.

## Scoring and records

Every crumb is worth 10 points, and clearing a pantry adds a 250-point bonus. In a pantry run, each new pantry uses the same maze with quicker cats.

Records tracks your best score, deepest pantry reached, lifetime tallies, and challenge results.

## The practice pantry

The practice pantry has only the ginger cat, moving slowly, and getting caught costs no whiskers. It is a relaxed way to get a feel for the maze and the cats.

## Challenges

Challenges give every player the exact same mazes for each period. You get 3 whiskers per run, and losing your last whisker ends the run with the points you have earned so far.

Tonight's pantry is the daily challenge: one 9 by 9 maze with the ginger cat and the tabby cat, refreshed every day.

Five pantries is the weekly challenge: five 9 by 9 mazes in a row with quicker cats in each pantry, refreshed every Monday.

The big larder is the monthly challenge: one 13 by 13 maze with the ginger, tabby, and grey cats, with hints turned off. A new maze arrives on the first of every month.

Choosing Leave the challenge from the pause menu stops without counting the run, so you can come back and try again later.

## Special nights

Two special pantries open on the title menu only on their own nights. Each plays like a pantry run with bonus treats hidden among the crumbs and keeps its own best score, so the main pantry board stays untouched. Once a special night ends, Play again on the results screen lets you know and will not start another run. You eat a treat by stepping onto the crumb in its cell. Sniffing with Space marks the nearest treat right after the crumb cues and names where it is hiding, S counts the treats left, and H points toward the nearest one.

Leftovers pantry runs from the day before Thanksgiving to 2 days after, hiding 4 turkey scraps worth 50 points each in every pantry.

Bin night opens on the first Tuesday of every month, when the kitchen bin is full before collection, hiding a cheese rind worth 100 points in every pantry.

## Controls

Arrows: move one cell. Hold to keep moving.

Space: sniff for the nearest crumbs.

Tab: freeze in place so the nearest cat loses your scent.

S: check status, listing each cat's position, crumbs, treats, and whiskers left. Press S again right away for freezes, score, and current maze.

R: repeat the last line.

H: find the way to the nearest safe crumb, direction first.

F1: help for what you are doing right now. F2 stops speech.

Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title becomes Leave the challenge.

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's instructions follow your bindings.
