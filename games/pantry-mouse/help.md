## About the game

Eat every crumb to clear the pantry maze. Each new pantry has quicker cats. The ginger cat starts in pantry 1, and the tabby joins in pantry 2. A cat on your cell costs a whisker. Lose all 3 and the run ends.

## Moving

The arrows move you one cell up, down, left or right. Hold an arrow to keep moving at a steady pace. You eat crumbs by moving onto them. If you hit a wall, the game names the open ways out of your cell, such as "Open: up and left."

The cats sleep until your first step in each pantry.

## Finding crumbs

Space sniffs. It marks the nearest 3 crumbs with directional cues, nearest first, then says how many crumbs are left.

H names the way to the nearest crumb that no cat is close to, then how many steps it is, such as "Up, 3 steps." "No safe crumb" means every crumb left is near a cat. H does not work in the monthly challenge.

## The cats

Each cat has a position cue when it is within 5 steps. The game names the cat and its direction on the first encounter. F1 during play explains how each cat moves.

The ginger cat takes the shortest route to you.

The tabby cat wanders at random until it is 4 steps away, then chases like the ginger cat.

The grey cat wanders like the tabby cat and only joins the monthly challenge.

The cat-close warning means a cat is within 2 steps. The first 3 times in a run, the warning is spoken too, such as "Ginger cat close, right." The direction is the way towards the cat, so go another way.

## Freezing

Tab freezes you briefly. The nearest cat loses you until just after the freeze ends. Other cats still hunt, and one that walks into you still catches you. The thaw cue means you can move again. You have 3 freezes in each pantry.

## Whiskers and catches

A catch costs a whisker. Then you and the cats go back to the start, and the cats wait 2 seconds before they move again. "Cats moving" says the wait is over. In the practice pantry a catch costs nothing.

## Status

S reads the status in the same order every time: each cat's place from you in cells, such as "ginger cat 3 cells left and 1 up", then crumbs, treats and whiskers left. Press S again straight away for your freezes, your score and the pantry.

## Pace

The cats and your freeze stand still while a line is still being spoken. An arrow pressed meanwhile moves you at once, and a held arrow steps on once the line ends. A line you ask for again and again stops holding after a while. This holds at every pace and in every mode.

Pace in Settings sets how fast the cats move: relaxed, normal or quick. Normal is the default and a little slower than quick. Quick keeps the original timings, and relaxed runs at half speed. The cats, your freezes and the waits between pantries follow the pace.

A change applies from your next run. A run or a challenge keeps the pace it started at until it ends.

Relaxed scores are saved separately, including challenges and special nights. Normal and quick share their scores. Your deepest pantry counts at every pace.

## Scoring and records

Each crumb is 10 points. Clearing a pantry adds 250 points. In the pantry run, each new pantry is the same maze with quicker cats.

Records keeps your best score, the deepest pantry you reached, your tallies, and your challenge results.

## The practice pantry

The practice pantry has only the ginger cat, moving slowly, and a catch costs no whisker. Use it to learn the maze and the cats.

## Challenges

Every player gets the same mazes for each challenge period. Each run has 3 whiskers, and losing the last ends it with the points so far.

Tonight's pantry, the daily: one 9 by 9 maze with the ginger cat and the tabby cat. A new one comes every day.

Five pantries, the weekly: five 9 by 9 mazes in a row, the cats quicker in each. A new set comes every Monday.

The big larder, the monthly: one 13 by 13 maze with the ginger, tabby and grey cats, and no hints. A new one comes on the first of every month.

Leave the challenge, on the pause menu, stops without counting the run, so you can try again later.

## Special nights

Two pantries open on the title only on their own nights. Each is the pantry run with a treat hidden among the crumbs, and each keeps its own best score, so the pantry's board never changes. Once a pantry has closed, Play again on its results says so and does not start it. A treat is eaten with the crumb in its cell. Space plays the nearest treat after the crumb cues, from where it hides, and names where it is. S counts the treats left, and H names the way to the nearest one.

Leftovers pantry, from the day before Thanksgiving to 2 days after: 4 turkey scraps in every pantry, 50 points each.

Bin night, on the first Tuesday of every month: the kitchen bin is full before collection, and a cheese rind waits in every pantry, 100 points.

## Controls

Arrows: move one cell. Hold to keep moving.

Space: sniff for the nearest crumbs.

Tab: freeze, so the nearest cat loses you.

S: status. Where each cat is, then crumbs, treats and whiskers left. Press S again straight away for freezes, score and which maze you are in.

R: repeat the last line.

H: the way to the nearest safe crumb, direction first.

F1: help for what you are doing right now. F2 stops speech.

Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title is Leave the challenge.

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's spoken instructions follow your bindings.
