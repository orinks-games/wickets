# Safecracker

## About the game

Ten safes, from office lockbox to Crown vault. Crack each by ear: turn the dial, hear the tumbler, set each wheel. No rush unless you pick time attack.

## The dial

The dial runs from 0 to 99 and wraps round. Left turns it one number down and Right one number up. Hold either to keep turning. Up spins the dial ten numbers up and Down spins it ten numbers down.

Every number gives a soft click. Every tenth number, 10, 20, 30 and so on, gives a firmer double click, and zero gives a deep notch, so you can count your way round. A spin makes a quick ratchet. Tab speaks the number under the mark, and S gives the full status.

## Finding a wheel's number

Every safe has between two and five wheels, and each wheel has its own secret number. Turn the dial and listen. When the dial reaches the number, the tumbler drops with a deep, solid clunk, just after the click. Press Space to set the wheel: a latch and a rising chime answer, and the next wheel is waiting.

If a spin of ten clunks, the number is somewhere in the ten you just passed. Step back one number at a time until it clunks again, then set it. Under the direction rule, from the jeweller's safe on, spin back instead and step forward the right way.

## Wrong sets and strikes

Set the wrong number and the wheel springs back with a twang. That is a strike. Three strikes on one safe and the relocker fires: the safe jams for good and the alarm rings. You can always try the safe again, with new numbers.

## Hints

H hints how far and which way: "very close" is 1 or 2 numbers away, "close" within 6, "a little way off" within 20, and "far off" more than 20. Each safe allows only a few hints, and each costs points. In time attack a hint also costs time.

## Building noises

From the hotel strongbox on, the building makes its own sounds: knocking pipes, creaking floors and a distant clock. They always come from off to your left or right. The dial and its tumbler are always straight in front of you.

## False gates

From the pawnbroker's safe on, each wheel has false gates: a hollow, rattling tick at wrong numbers. Ignore them. Only the deep clunk is the true tumbler. The sound check lets you hear both side by side.

## The direction rule

From the jeweller's safe on, the safes behave like real combination dials. Wheel 1 is found turning right, wheel 2 turning left, wheel 3 right again, and so on. The tumbler only clunks when you are turning the right way, and you must reach the number turning that way before you set it. If you overshoot, turn back past the number and come forward again the right way. The same goes for a spin that clunks: stepping back one number at a time is silent, because that is the wrong way, so spin back the ten you just passed and then step forward the right way one number at a time until it clunks. Setting the right number reached the wrong way is a strike. Status tells you which way the current wheel wants and which way you last turned.

## The night guard

From the bank manager's safe on, a night guard walks the corridor. You hear his footsteps coming for several seconds, growing louder. When they stop he is at the door, listening, and his keys jingle. Keep still: do not turn the dial or set a wheel until you hear his footsteps walk away. Status, repeat, Tab and hints make no noise, so you can use them while he listens.

The first noise he hears makes him suspicious: "He heard something!" He stays suspicious until the safe opens, and each time he comes back the game says so in words. The second noise and you are caught. A key held down as he stops counts only once: let go, and only a fresh press can make that second noise. On the bank manager's safe the game also tells you in words when he comes and goes. After that, listen for him, and press S if you are unsure where he is.

## Scoring and best times

Every wheel is worth 100 points. Crack a safe quickly for a time bonus. Each strike costs 30 points and each hint 20. Each safe keeps your best time and best score, and Records on the title reads them all, with your challenge results.

## Modes

Play, the first row of the title, opens the first safe you have not yet cracked. Safe select replays any safe you have reached. Crack safe 3 to open time attack, where any safe you have cracked comes with a clock. A tick warns you at 1 minute, 30 seconds and 10 seconds left. The clock stops while the guard listens at the door. Practice dial gives you endless single wheels with nothing to lose, which is the best way to learn the sound of the clunk. Sound check plays each sound you need at the safe, with its name. Challenges holds the daily, weekly and monthly jobs.

After a safe, the results menu offers the next safe when you opened one, Play again or Try again, Hear the results again, and Back to the title. Escape does the same as Back to the title.

## Bonus safes

Two bonus safes open on the title on their nights only, after Safe select. On other nights their rows say locked and tell you when they open. Anyone can open them, however far you have got. Each keeps its own best score, apart from the ten safes, and Records reads them once you have cracked them.

The unlucky safe is on every Friday the 13th. It has four wheels, each with a false gate, and building noises. One of the wheels is always 13. A black cat sleeps on top, and she gives a soft, short yowl just after the clunk at 13, so you know that wheel when you find it. She never yowls over a click: turn or spin again and she falls quiet at once, even mid-yowl.

The blackout safe is on every new moon night, and the night either side, about once a month. It has three wheels, each with a false gate. The power is cut, so the room hum gives way to rain on the windows, and in the dark the tens give the plain click instead of the double click. The zero notch still sounds, so count from zero, or press Tab for the number. S reminds you of either safe's rule.

## Challenges

Challenges on the title holds three jobs that are the same for every player, built from the date. Each row names the period and the job, says what it is this time, then gives your record: not played yet, or your best and your streak. Enter reads the briefing first: the rule, the twist, your record and when the next one comes. Enter on the last page starts the job, and Escape on any page goes back without starting. Your result counts when the job ends, by opening every safe or losing one. Leave the challenge, on the pause menu, does not count, so you can come back and try again. After the job, the results menu offers the same job again, Hear the results again, and Back to the challenges.

Tonight's job, the daily: one safe of three or four wheels, with one twist. Quiet tumblers make the clunk as faint as the Crown vault's. A nervous guard comes often and listens long. The direction rule puts the real dial's rule on. Crowded gates give every wheel three false gates. No hints means only your ears. A busy building knocks and creaks every few seconds. A new job comes every day.

The vault of three, the weekly: three safes in a row, the outer safe, the inner safe under the direction rule, and the strongroom safe with a guard who comes unannounced, all with the week's twist. One relocker guards the whole vault: three strikes across all three safes jam it, and three hints are shared. When the twist is no hints, there are none. Each safe you open banks its points, even if a later one beats you. A new vault comes every Monday.

The Crown heist, the monthly: a story event. Through the curator's safe for the key card, then the five wheels of the Crown vault, with a guard on the corridor between. One clock of 5 minutes runs for the whole heist. It stops while the guard listens, and a hint costs five seconds. Open both for 3 points for every second left. A new heist comes on the first of every month.

Challenge scores are 100 a wheel, less 30 a strike and 20 a hint. The daily and weekly add a bonus for a quick crack. A heavy door and a bell mean a safe is open and the next is waiting. S tells you which safe of the job you are on, your banked points and the twist. After the job you hear your score, your best and your streak.

## Controls

Left and Right: turn the dial one number down or up.

Up and Down: spin the dial ten numbers up or down.

Space: set the wheel.

Tab: say the number under the mark.

S: status. The guard if he is coming, at the door or leaving, then the number, the wheel and which way it was found. If S does not name the guard, the corridor is quiet. Press S again straight away for strikes, hints, time and the job.

R: repeat the last instruction.

H: a hint.

F1: help for what you are doing right now. F2 stops speech.

Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title is Leave the challenge.

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's spoken instructions follow your bindings.
