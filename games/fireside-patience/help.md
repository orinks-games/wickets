# Fireside Patience

## About the game

Settle in by a cabin fire for a classic game of solitaire. Your goal is to sort all 52 cards onto four suit foundations, building each suit from ace up to king.

Every relaxed deal is guaranteed winnable, and you have unlimited undo to backtrack whenever you want. There is no clock and no move limit, so you cannot lose.

## The row of places

The table is laid out as a single row of 12 places: piles 1 to 7, then the stock, then the foundations for hearts, diamonds, clubs, and spades. Left and Right move along the row, naming each place's top card as you land on it. You stop at either end of the row.

## Piles

Each pile starts with face-down cards underneath one face-up card on top. Piles build down in alternating colours: a black 7 goes on a red 8, and a red queen goes on a black king. Only a king can move onto an empty pile.

When you move the last face-up card off a pile, the card beneath it flips face up and is named. Up steps deeper into a pile's face-up run, and Down steps back toward the top card.

## Moving cards

Space picks up the card under you along with every card stacked on top of it. Move to your target spot and press Space again to place them. If a move is not allowed, the rule is explained, such as "The 9 of spades cannot go on the 9 of hearts. It needs a red 10."

You keep holding the cards after a blocked move so you can try another spot. While you hold cards, valid destinations announce "Fits here." If you carry a single card over the wrong foundation, its matching suit is named, such as "Goes to spades." Pressing Space on the spot you picked the cards up from puts them back.

## The stock

Tab turns one card face up from the stock, and Space picks up the turned-up card when you are on the stock. Once the stock runs out, Tab flips the waste back over so you can cycle through it as often as you like.

Pressing Tab while holding cards returns them first. In draw three, Tab turns 3 cards at a time and only the top card can be played.

## Foundations

Each suit has its own foundation, built one card at a time from ace up to king. Uncover cards in the piles and turn the stock to find the aces.

Pressing Space on any foundation sends a single card straight to its own suit. Once every card is face up and the stock is cleared, the remaining cards fly up to the foundations on their own.

## Undo and getting stuck

Backspace takes back your last move as many times as you like, flipping revealed cards back down as well. While you are holding cards, Backspace puts them back where they came from.

If no useful moves remain, you are told so. Because every relaxed deal is winnable from the start, undoing far enough always opens a path forward. For a fresh deal, press Escape, then Back to the title, then Play.

## Hints

H suggests a useful next move, such as "The 4 of clubs in pile 2 can go on the 5 of hearts in pile 6." Whenever a winning path exists from your current table, hints follow it so you never walk into a dead end. When the needed card sits deeper in the stock, the hint tells you to turn the stock. Hints are free.

## Status and the table

S reports any cards you are holding and your current spot on the table. Press S again right away for your foundation count, cards left in the stock, face-down cards remaining, and move count, always in that order.

R reads the whole table from left to right: the top card of each pile, the stock, and all four foundations.

## Scoring and records

Every card move and every turn of the stock counts as one move. Undoing a move takes it back off your count. Winning a deal scores 1000 points minus 1 point per move.

Records track your relaxed deals won, fewest moves, and best score.

## Challenges

Every player gets the same winnable deals. The daily, Tonight's deal, is one draw one deal, fresh each day. The weekly, Draw three week, is one draw three deal, fresh each Monday. The monthly, Five by the fire, is 5 draw one deals in a row with their scores added together.

Your challenge result counts once the last card reaches its foundation. Picking Leave the challenge from the pause menu cancels the run so you can try again later.

## Calendar events

Rabbit rabbit deal opens on the 1st of every month, named for the old charm of saying "rabbit rabbit" for a lucky month. On other days its title row stays locked and says when it opens next.

Every player gets the same winnable deal that day, and turning the stock costs no moves, so your score counts only the cards you move and S notes "turns are free". It keeps its own high-score board. Once the day passes, Play again on the results notes that the Rabbit rabbit deal has ended and returns you to the title.

## Sound check

Sound check on the title plays every card and table cue you need during a deal.

## Controls

Left and Right: move along the piles, the stock, and the foundations.

Up and Down: step deeper into a pile's face-up cards, or back toward the top card.

Space: pick up the cards under you, or place the cards you are holding.

Tab: turn the stock.

Backspace: undo your last move, or put back the cards you are holding. This key belongs to Fireside Patience and cannot be changed in Settings.

Home and End: jump straight to the first pile or the last foundation. These keys belong to Fireside Patience too.

S: what you hold and where you are. Press S twice for the full table counts.

R: read the whole table.

H: suggest a useful move.

F1: help for what you are doing right now. F2 stops speech.

Escape: pause, with Resume, Settings, Help, Back to the title, and Back to the lobby. In a challenge, Back to the title becomes Leave the challenge.

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's instructions follow your bindings.
