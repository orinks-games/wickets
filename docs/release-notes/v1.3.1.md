# Wicket's 1.3.1

This patch makes instructions and settings clearer. Your saves and records
remain compatible.

- Belfry explains the secret bell sequence, guesses and feedback before
  introducing its bell-ringing terms. Its tutorial clarifies how Plain and
  Village differ.
- Neon Rail explains which traffic to meet and which hazards to avoid. The
  tutorial correctly identifies slowdown pulses and priority overrides as
  limited-charge tools; boost recharges between uses. Empty tools now say
  that no charges remain.
- Lantern Lake gives a clearer first paddling task and explains its shoreline
  landmarks. Rooftop Garden explains how to recover from watering mistakes.
- Story help explains how to find and complete the suggested activity.
- Game preferences is now called Story and event games, with clearer help
  explaining that excluded games remain available in Play.
- Freewheel calls its default speech setting once per session and explains
  when that session ends. Left and Right now move through its choices in
  opposite directions.
- Cupid's Quiver labels its speech options Spoken aim and Spoken targets.

No game rules, scores or unlock requirements have changed.
