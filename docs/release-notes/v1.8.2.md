# Wicket's 1.8.2

A few fixes before the holidays. Your saves and records carry over.

## Fixes

- Leaderboards: a score posted right after another one now waits its
  turn and posts, instead of saying "Not posted."
- Leaderboards: a board that can't load now says so, instead of saying
  your run was not accepted.
- Holiday games: when you are flying high with a spire ahead, H now says
  to stay high instead of telling you to dip.
- Holiday games: a weather line no longer comes round again a few seconds
  after it was said.
