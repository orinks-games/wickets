# Wicket's 1.6.0

This update adds Order Up!, a new game at Chef Crumb's sweets counter. Timed
games now wait while a line is being spoken, and each has a Pace setting.
Your saves and records carry over.

## Order Up!

A new game. Chef Crumb runs a sweets counter on TV, and you work it.
Customers call their orders. Scoop the ice cream, pour the sauce, add the
toppings and cook on the grill, then serve each order before the customer
loses patience. Crumb judges every order and gives each episode up to 3
stars.

- The show runs for 3 seasons of 8 episodes. Each episode brings something
  new to the counter, and each season ends with a dinner rush.
- Stars add new items to the menu board. Late in the show you can build
  and name your own sundae, and customers start ordering it.
- The practice counter has no clock and keeps no score.
- A daily lunch rush, a weekly critic's visit and a monthly show that
  changes the rules, such as a power cut or a food truck.
- Sundae Thursday on the third Thursday of each month, Peanut Butter Day on
  24 January, and Crumb's Ice Cream Social on the third Sunday of July.
- Settings can leave out Crumb's bleeped lines and turn his chatter down.

## Pace and speech

- Game clocks now wait while a line is still being spoken, so a slower
  voice no longer costs you time. This works in every mode, including
  challenges and events.
- Settings has a Pace row for each timed game: relaxed, normal or quick.
  Quick is the timing these games had before. Normal is a little slower and
  is the default. Relaxed is half speed. Order Up! starts on relaxed.
- Stars, unlocks and progress count at every pace. Normal and quick share
  bests. Relaxed keeps its own bests.
- A key you press while the clock is waiting is never lost.
- Time spent waiting for speech still counts toward time scores, so asking
  for status cannot improve a time.
- A score of 0 no longer goes on a board.
- Pace is in Order Up!, Harvest Feast, Signal Box, Winter Wonderland, Neon
  Rail, Nevermore Manor, Tightrope, Soft Landing, Safecracker, Freewheel,
  Pantry Mouse, Sonar Dive, Basket Run and Tide Pools. It replaces Harvest
  Feast's relaxed course pace. A relaxed choice you saved there and its
  bests carry over.

## Settings

- Settings opens on a short list, such as Speech, Sound, Display and
  Controls. Enter opens one, and Escape comes back.
- From a game, the first entry is that game, with its own options and
  Units. Settings from the pause menu works the same way.
- Units and Story and event games are under Games in the lobby's Settings.

## Rooftop Garden

- Four new seeds: strawberry, chilli, rose and jasmine. Each one unlocks in
  its own way and adds a new voice to the song. Strawberries root runners,
  chillies follow the sun, roses get few aphids but grow leggy, and jasmine
  sings only from late afternoon to dawn.
- Challenge gardens keep the first six seeds.

## Holiday specials

- A holiday special can be found in its season and for two weeks after.
  Start a run or a practice and it stays in your list all year.
- Finding a new special needs the date from the internet. When Wicket's has
  no date, Seasonal games has a Check for holiday specials row.

## Soundtrack

- Export album now saves Ogg files instead of WAV. They are much smaller,
  and music players show each track's title, album and number.

## Keys and help

- A key that does nothing in a game is now silent. It no longer says it is
  not a valid key.
- Help pages now cover how to play. The sounds to learn are in each game's
  sound check.
- Tightrope's help and tutorial explain that you let go of the key between
  balance presses.
