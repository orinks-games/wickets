# Wicket's 1.6.0

This update sorts Settings into categories, adds Report a problem and
four new Rooftop Garden seeds, and puts the help and tutorials into
plainer words. Your settings, saves and records carry over.

## Settings

- Settings opens on a short list, such as Speech, Sound, Display and
  Controls. Enter opens one, and Escape comes back.
- From a game, the first entry is that game, with its own options and
  Units. Settings from the pause menu works the same way.
- Units and Story and event games are under Games in the lobby's Settings.
- Online account connects an orinks.net account. Nothing in the parlor
  needs one yet.

## Reporting a problem

- Report a problem, in Settings, sends the game log to orinks.net and
  opens the report form in your browser with the log already attached.
  Write what happened, add your email address if you want a reply, and
  send it. A report sent from inside a game names that game.
- If Wicket's closed without quitting the last time it ran, the next
  start asks once whether to report it. Don't ask again turns the
  question off; Settings has it under Ask to report a problem after a
  crash.

## Rooftop Garden

- Four new seeds: strawberry, chilli, rose and jasmine. Strawberry comes
  at 12 harvests, chilli after 5 filled requests, rose with the trellis
  keepsake, and jasmine with your first six-voice chorus.
- Strawberries root runners, chillies follow the sun, roses get few
  aphids but grow leggy, and jasmine sings only from late afternoon to
  dawn. Challenge gardens keep the first six seeds.

## Soundtrack

- Export album now saves Ogg files instead of WAV. They are much smaller,
  and music players show each track's title, album and number.

## Speech

- At a slow speech rate, a line you need to hear now plays in full
  before its clock runs on: Harvest Feast's recipe calls and due lines,
  Winter Wonderland's first gift and edge warnings, Safecracker's guard,
  Pantry Mouse's first thaw and a ghost's arrival in Nevermore Manor.

## Help and tutorials

- Help pages, tutorials and the help rows in play are shorter and
  plainer.

## Updates

- On Windows, an update that could not swap in while a file was still
  open no longer gets rolled back and offered again. It finishes at the
  next start.
