# Wicket's 1.6.0

This update adds a Pace setting for timed games, the Wish Desk in Winter
Wonderland, four new Rooftop Garden seeds, a sorted Settings menu and a
way to report a problem from the game. Your saves and records carry over.

## Pace and speech

- Game clocks hold while a line is still being spoken, in every mode,
  challenges and events included.
- Settings has a Pace row for each timed game: relaxed, normal or quick.
  Quick is the old timing. Normal is a little slower and is the default.
  Relaxed is half speed. A change applies from the next run.
- Pace covers Harvest Feast, Signal Box, Winter Wonderland's Gift Sort,
  Neon Rail, Nevermore Manor, Tightrope, Soft Landing, Safecracker,
  Freewheel, Pantry Mouse, Sonar Dive, Basket Run and Tide Pools.
- Stars, unlocks and progress count at every pace. Normal and quick share
  bests. Relaxed keeps its own bests, challenge results included.
- A key pressed while the clock is held still counts, at once or as soon
  as the clock runs.
- Status, repeat and hint hold the clock only up to a limit that refills
  as you play, so they cannot stop a danger for good.
- Time held for speech still counts in time scores.
- A score of 0 goes on no board.
- Harvest Feast's Relaxed course pace switch is replaced by Pace. A saved
  relaxed choice and its bests carry over.

## Winter Wonderland

- The Wish Desk is a new half of the game, with no clock. A child's letter
  is read out. Walk the shelf with Left and Right, shake a box with Space,
  and hold Space to wrap the box that matches the wish.
- There are 5 stacks of letters. Stacks 1 and 2 say how each wish sounds;
  from stack 3 you work it out.
- Muddled post comes on the fourth Wednesday of every month. Late post
  adds two double-value letters to your first stack each evening.
- Story mode, the daily and the monthly challenge now end at the desk.
  Midnight Countdown keeps its title row and rhythm practice.
- The tutorial teaches the desk.

## Rooftop Garden

- Four new seeds: strawberry, chilli, rose and jasmine, each with 3
  instruments. Strawberry roots runners, chilli is thirsty, rose is slow,
  and jasmine sings only from late afternoon until dawn.
- The seed shed says what unlocks each locked seed.
- A chorus of 6 or more voices that beats your last one plays the chorus
  stinger. A ten-voice chorus is possible toward evening.
- Old gardens get any new seeds they have already earned.

## Seasonal games

- A seasonal game stays listed for 14 days after its season.
- Once you start a run or a practice, it is yours to keep, and it opens
  offline after its season.
- New seasonal games are found by the internet date. With no connection,
  Seasonal games has a Check for holiday specials row.

## Settings

- Settings opens on a short list, such as Speech, Sound, Display and
  Controls. Enter opens one, and Escape comes back.
- From a game, the first entry is that game, with its own options and
  Units. Settings from the pause menu works the same way.

## Report a problem

- Report a problem, at the end of Settings, sends the game log to
  orinks.net and opens the report form in your browser.
- From a game's pause menu, the report names that game.
- After a run that closed unexpectedly, the next start offers to send a
  report once. Settings can turn that offer off.

## Help and controls

- Help, tutorials and Settings help use plainer wording, and help text no
  longer describes sounds. The sound check still names each sound.
- Keys a game does not use are silent everywhere.
- Birdsong Trail's help now says the game speaks a line when you leave
  the path. The Echo's help now says the wobble stops when the pipe is in
  tune.

## Soundtrack

- Export album saves Ogg files instead of WAV. They are much smaller, and
  music players show each track's title, album and number.
