# Wicket's 1.7.0 beta 1

This beta adds two new Cozy games, Larkfield and Little Rapids. It also
has everything in 1.6.0. Your saves and records carry over.

## Larkfield

A new Cozy game. You're the first farmer in an empty hamlet by a river.
Dig and sow the fields, fish, chop wood and put up buildings. Settlers come
to the gate asking to stay, each with a past trade, two traits and a dream
to finish. Keep everyone fed and cheerful, and the hamlet grows. Every
settler plays an instrument in the hamlet's tune from wherever they're
working.

- Choose a pace in Settings. At the steady and lively paces your hamlet
  meets dangers like fires, foxes and floods, and a settler kept glum too
  long leaves. At the gentle pace there are no dangers. Nobody ever dies.
- Four seasons with festivals, challenges, and a keepsake shelf.
- A barge fair on the third Tuesday of each month, and music night on the
  fourth Sunday.

## Little Rapids

A new Cozy game. You steer a paper boat down a stream and catch what
floats toward you on the beat. Every catch plays a note, and the river's
song adds instruments as your combo grows. A bump costs your combo but
never ends the run.

- Four streams, opened with stars, and the endless river once you have
  reached a river mouth. Drift has no score at all.
- Fold new boats from paper scraps, and collect trinkets for the keepsake
  shelf.
- A duck parade on the second Monday of each month, and a lantern float on
  the third Friday.

Please tell us what feels too hard, too easy or unclear in either game.
