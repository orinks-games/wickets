# Wicket's 1.7.1

This patch fixes the Fireside Patience tutorial. Your saves and records
carry over.

- The tutorial now continues straight away after you put the 2 of hearts
  up. Looking around no longer delays the next step.
- The tutorial now shows where the other aces are and how to turn the
  stock back over when it runs out. It ends with the winning rule: build
  all 4 foundations from ace to king.
- Help for Fireside Patience now includes where the aces start.
