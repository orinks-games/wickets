# Wicket's 1.3.2

This patch fixes exporting from the Soundtrack. Your saves and records
remain compatible.

- Exported albums now go in a Music folder inside the Wicket's folder,
  not your own Music folder. Each album gets a folder of its own, and the
  game says where it saved the album when the export is done.
- The lobby's time-of-day pieces (Parlor rag, Morning, Afternoon, Evening
  and Small hours) are listed from the start as not heard yet. Play one in
  the Soundtrack menu and it counts as heard, so you can export Wicket's
  album without opening the parlor at every hour.
- The Pause theme track is gone from Wicket's album. Every game has its own
  pause music, so that track could never be heard, and the album could
  never be exported.
