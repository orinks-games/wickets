# Wicket's 1.9.0 alpha 2

## Pebbles online

- My games is now in your language, along with whose move it is and the
  score in each match.
- Online matches count in Pebbles' records, and Play online says how many
  you have won.
- Leaving a match now names the player who wins it.
- A tied score now reads as "tied at 1" in place of "1 all".
