# Wicket's 1.6.0 alpha 4

Your runs now leave a song behind. Your settings and saves carry over.

## Soundtrack

- In a game whose music is made as you play (Rooftop Garden, Lantern
  Lake, Larkfield, Little Rapids, Snowfall Skate, and every game with a
  score that follows the action, such as Tightrope or Tide Pools), each
  run's music is kept as a song of that run: the voices joining and
  leaving when they did, the fades as they happened. Nothing from a
  pause or a menu gets in.
- Each game's songs are in an album of their own, such as Your Rooftop
  Garden songs, named for the time of day and the date. The last 30 of
  each game are kept. The Lantern Lake nights and Rooftop Garden
  sessions kept before this build stay in those albums until newer songs
  push them out.
- A run's results offer Make a sound file of this run's song, which
  goes in your Music folder. In any album that exports, Shift and Enter
  on a track saves that one track.
- Keep your songs, under Sound in Settings, turns the keeping off. Songs
  already kept stay.
- Midnight Countdown's instrument notes now play as music, so a night at
  the party is kept as a song too.
