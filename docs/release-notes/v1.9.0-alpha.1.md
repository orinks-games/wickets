# Wicket's 1.9.0 alpha 1

This alpha adds Pebbles, a new Table game you can play against the
parlour's regulars or online against another player. It also has
everything in 1.8.0. Your saves and records carry over.

## Pebbles

A pile of pebbles sits between you and your opponent. Each turn takes 1, 2
or 3, and whoever takes the last pebble loses the round. A match is first
to 3 rounds.

- Three regulars sit down at the corner table. Dot takes at random, Alf
  plays the end perfectly from 9 pebbles down, and Marta plays perfectly.
- The tutorial plays a whole match against Dot, step by step.
- H names a safe take and why. Tab counts the pile.
- Challenges: Today's piles every day against Marta, Twin piles on Mondays
  with two piles on the table, and The long table each month, 9 rounds with
  piles of up to 60.
- Knock night, on the first Thursday of the month: 9 rounds against all
  three regulars, and each player may knock once a round to pass the turn.
- The match has its own music, which follows the pile down.

## Pebbles online

- Play online, on the Pebbles title, plays a match against another player,
  as a guest or with your orinks.net account.
- Create a game gives you an invite code to share. Join with a code takes
  one. Play with anyone joins an open game, or opens one for the next
  player.
- The player who joined chooses the seats in round 1, then the loser of
  each round chooses.
- My games lists your matches, says whose move it is, and opens a match
  again where it left off. Escape goes back to the room and keeps your
  seat.
- Leave the match from the room, or take more than 3 days over a move, and
  the other player wins it.

Please tell us if a move is slow to reach the other player, or if a match
ever disagrees with what you heard.
