# Wicket's 1.5.0

This update brings a built-in voice, an optional visual display, deeper
Rooftop Garden play and clearer Help across the parlor. Your saves and
records carry over.

## Speech

- When no screen reader or system speech is available, Wicket's speaks with
  its own built-in voice. Settings, Speech voice offers automatic selection
  or one of six built-in voices. While the built-in voice is in use,
  Settings also shows its volume, and its rate moves in 1 percent steps.
- System speech engines that have no voices installed are skipped, so the
  built-in voice takes over instead of leaving the game silent.
- If you hear nothing at all, press Control, Shift and V together on any
  screen. It switches between your chosen built-in voice and automatic
  selection, and the game remembers the choice.

## Visual display

- The window now shows game scenes, menus, status text and captions
  alongside speech and sound. Live tutorials show their instructions next
  to the scene.
- Settings can turn the display off, enlarge text up to 200 percent, use
  high-contrast text and stop decorative motion.
- Hold Control and press Page Up or Page Down to scroll the screen text.
  Add Shift to go back through earlier captions.

## Rooftop Garden

- Every seed now comes in varieties, each with its own instrument, thirst,
  growth pace and harvest. A variety plays its part of the song on its own
  instrument.
- Harvest a plant next to a different plant in bloom and the two may cross
  into new seeds. Now and then a plant throws something new by itself. A
  variety you harvest five times becomes an heirloom.
- Neighbours ask for crops with a certain trait. Press N to hear what they
  want. Filling a request earns a star, and sometimes gift seeds.
- Stars unlock ten keepsakes, and each one changes how the garden plays: a
  bird bath that brings sparrows to eat aphids, a rain barrel, a beehive for more
  crosses, a cold frame that keeps one planter's soil from drying out, a
  potting bench for crossing two plants of your choice, and more.
- The seed shed lists the varieties you have found, where you can hear each
  one's instrument, plus your keepsakes and open requests. Records has a new
  Collection page. Start over keeps your varieties and keepsakes.
- Challenge gardens still use plain seeds, so everyone plays the same
  garden.
- A ripe plant that has wilted now says it is ripe, so you know Tab can
  still harvest it.

## Freewheel

- Pedalling now sounds like a bike chain, not a motor. Coasting plays each
  bike's freewheel ticking, slowing down as you slow down. Road bike,
  mountain bike and BMX each sound different.
- Freewheel's sounds are evened out, so warnings stand out and no sound is
  much louder or quieter than the rest.

## Menus

- Seasonal games are listed in calendar order: today's events first, then
  each game by how soon its holiday comes round.
- Relaxing games are now called Cozy games. Your choices in Story and event
  games carry over.

## Neon Rail

- The workshop sells more parts as your rank rises, and cold starts earn
  new upgrades for every run. Routes you have cleared already come up less
  often.
- In the endless rail, End run here between sectors finishes the run, so it
  is paid and the sector you reached counts.

## Help and tutorials

- Help pages and tutorial lines across the parlor are shorter and clearer.
- Harvest Feast's tutorial no longer marks a dish down when you press Space
  while it is still talking.

## Updates

- When an update is found at start, Wicket's offers it at the main menu.
  Check for updates in Settings still works too.
- Your update channel stays as you set it after an update.
- Installing an update is faster, starting with the update after this one.
