# Wicket's 1.8.1

This update lets you play Wicket's in 11 more languages, keeps your
progress with your orinks.net account on every computer, and gives you a
player page on orinks.net. Posting a score to the leaderboards needs no
setup now. Your saves and records carry over.

## Languages

- Wicket's can now be played in Spanish, French, German, Brazilian
  Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Polish, Simplified Chinese, Japanese,
  Turkish and Vietnamese. Menus, Settings, every game, tutorials, help,
  results and story are all translated.
- To change it, open Settings, then Speech. Language is the first row.
  Automatic follows your computer's language.
- Your screen reader speaks the game in your language. The built-in voice
  and the holiday characters' voices stay in English.
- The first translations were made by an AI, so some lines will be wrong.
  Tell us at https://github.com/orinks-games/wickets/issues with the
  language, what the game said, and what it should say.

## Your progress on every computer

- When you sign in with your orinks.net account, your scores, unlocks and
  progress follow you to any computer you sign in on.
- If a computer already has progress of its own, Wicket's asks once
  whether to combine it with your account. Combining keeps your best
  scores and adds up your plays from both.
- Guests keep their progress on their own computer, as before.

## Your player page

- Signed-in players get a page on orinks.net with their best scores and
  the holiday specials they have beaten. You can hide your page if you
  prefer.

## Leaderboards

- Every ranked challenge now counts toward the leaderboards. When you
  choose to post a score, you join as a guest under a name the site
  picks, so there's nothing to set up first.

## Fixes

- Safecracker: at the end of a heist challenge, the time left on the
  clock now matches the bonus points it gives.
