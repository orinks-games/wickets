# Wicket's 1.7.0

This update adds Order Up!, a new game at Chef Crumb's sweets counter. It
also has everything in 1.6.0. Your saves and records carry over.

## Order Up!

A new game. Chef Crumb runs a sweets counter on TV, and you work it.
Customers call their orders. Scoop the ice cream, pour the sauce, add the
toppings and cook on the grill, then serve each order before the customer
loses patience. Crumb judges every order and gives each episode up to 3
stars.

- The show runs for 3 seasons of 8 episodes. Each episode brings something
  new to the counter, and each season ends with a dinner rush.
- Stars add new items to the menu board. Late in the show you can build
  and name your own sundae, and customers start ordering it.
- The practice counter has no clock and keeps no score.
- A daily lunch rush, a weekly critic's visit and a monthly show that
  changes the rules, such as a power cut or a food truck.
- Sundae Thursday on the third Thursday of each month, Peanut Butter Day on
  24 January, and Crumb's Ice Cream Social on the third Sunday of July.
- Pace in Settings starts on relaxed. Clocks wait while a line is being
  spoken, as in the other timed games.
- Settings can leave out Crumb's bleeped lines and turn his chatter down.

## Story

- When you open an errand from Story and it leads into a lesson, the errand
  is spoken first, then the lesson.

## Signal Box

- On an event night, S gives the shift's numbers first. Press S again for
  the event. H no longer repeats it.
