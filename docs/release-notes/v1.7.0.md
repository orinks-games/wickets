# Wicket's 1.7.0

This update brings three new games, Order Up!, Larkfield and Little
Rapids, plus a new way to ride in Freewheel, a briefing before play, and
songs kept from your runs. Your saves and records carry over.

## New games

- Order Up!: run a sweets counter on a TV cooking show. Scoop, pour, top
  and grill each order, then serve it at the window before the customer
  gives up. Seasons of episodes, a practice counter, daily and weekly
  challenges, a different mode each month, and special days through the
  year.
- Larkfield, in Cozy games: build a small hamlet by a river. Farm, fish,
  chop wood and keep hens, sell to the barge, and settlers come to stay.
- Little Rapids, in Cozy games: sail a little boat down a stream and catch
  what floats along with the music. Four streams with stars to earn, an
  endless river, a drift with no score, and a boatyard.

## Freewheel

- Against the clock, under More rides: make delivery drops to add time,
  and ride until the clock runs out. Each district keeps its own best.
- A new title theme and a shorter title menu.

## Briefing before play

- Order Up!, Belfry and Dragon Dance open a short briefing before a run.
  It says what the run is, and the number keys play the sounds that run
  needs. Enter or Space starts.
- Settings in those games has a new choice, Briefing before play. Turn it
  off to start runs at once.

## Your songs

- In a game whose music is made as you play, each run's music is kept as
  a song of that run. Each game's songs are in an album of their own in
  the Soundtrack, and the last 30 are kept.
- A run's results offer Make a sound file of this run's song, which goes in
  your Music folder. Keep your songs, under Sound in Settings, turns the
  keeping off.

## Fixes

- Nevermore Manor's tutorial no longer gets stuck in the last room.
- Lamplighter, Pantry Mouse, Tide Pools and Nevermore Manor move each sound
  to its new place on every step.
- When you open an errand from Story and it leads into a lesson, the errand
  is spoken first, then the lesson.
- Signal Box: on an event night, S gives the shift's numbers first. Press
  S again for the event.
- Menus, results, in-play lines and story use plainer wording.
- Dragon Dance's challenges no longer give the run's length in seconds.
