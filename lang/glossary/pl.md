# Glossary: Polish

Wicket's speaks to the player in the second person singular, with "ty"
forms ("Masz nowy rekord"), as Polish games usually do. The register is
plain, warm and spoken: a friend in the parlor talking to you, not a
manual. The game does not know the player's gender, so prefer present
tense or an impersonal form ("Gra odblokowana") over a gendered past tense.

Key names (Enter, Escape, Space, Tab, Page Up, F1, the arrow keys Up, Down,
Left and Right, and the rest), game names and people's names stay in
English.
