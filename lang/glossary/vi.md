# Glossary: Vietnamese

Wicket's speaks standard, everyday Vietnamese that players from every
region understand. Menus, help and system lines call the player "bạn";
short commands drop the subject. Characters talking to the player use the
pair "tôi" and "bạn", and Mabel and Wicket call the player "chủ tiệm". The
register is plain, warm and spoken: a friend in the parlor talking to you,
not a manual. Vietnamese has no plural endings; write one form.

Key names (Enter, Escape, Space, Tab, Page Up, F1, the arrow keys Up, Down,
Left and Right, and the rest), game names and people's names stay in
English.
