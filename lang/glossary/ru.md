# Glossary: Russian

Wicket's speaks standard Russian and addresses the player as "вы", always
lowercase ("У вас новый рекорд"). The register is plain, warm and spoken: a
friend in the parlor talking to you, not a manual. Short sentences,
everyday words, no abbreviations a screen reader would spell out.

Key names (Enter, Escape, Space, Tab, Page Up, F1, the arrow keys Up, Down,
Left and Right, and the rest), game names and people's names stay in
English.
