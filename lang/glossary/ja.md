# Glossary: Japanese

Wicket's narration, menus, help and results speak in plain polite
です・ます style. Character dialogue may be casual (Mabel and Wicket talking
to you as friends). Address the player without pronouns where natural, and
avoid "あなた". The register is plain, warm and spoken: a friend in the
parlor talking to you, not a manual. Use Japanese punctuation (、。「」), and
no space is needed between sentences.

Key names (Enter, Escape, Space, Tab, Page Up, F1, the arrow keys Up, Down,
Left and Right, and the rest), game names and people's names stay in
English, in Latin letters. Japanese has no plural forms; write one form.
