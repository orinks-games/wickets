# Glossary: Simplified Chinese

Wicket's speaks standard Mainland Chinese and addresses the player as "你",
never "您", in narration, menus and character dialogue alike. The register
is plain, warm and spoken: a friend in the parlor talking to you, not a
manual. Characters may sound more casual than menus. Use full-width
punctuation (，。：？！“”), and no space is needed between sentences.

Key names (Enter, Escape, Space, Tab, Page Up, F1, the arrow keys Up, Down,
Left and Right, and the rest), game names and people's names stay in
English, in Latin letters. Chinese has no plural forms; write one form.
