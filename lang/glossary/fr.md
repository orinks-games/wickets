# Glossary: French

Wicket's speaks standard international French and addresses the player
with "tu". The register is plain, warm and spoken: a friend in the
parlor talking to you, not a manual. Short sentences, everyday words.

Key names (Enter, Escape, Space, Tab, Page Up, F1, the arrow keys Up, Down,
Left and Right, and the rest), game names and people's names stay in
English.
