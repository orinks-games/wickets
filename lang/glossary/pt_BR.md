# Glossary: Brazilian Portuguese

Wicket's speaks Brazilian Portuguese and addresses the player as "você",
with verbs in the third person ("Você tem um novo recorde"). The register
is plain, warm and spoken: a friend in the parlor talking to you, not a
manual. Short sentences, everyday words.

Key names (Enter, Escape, Space, Tab, Page Up, F1, the arrow keys Up, Down,
Left and Right, and the rest), game names and people's names stay in
English.
