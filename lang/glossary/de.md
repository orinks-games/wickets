# Glossary: German

Wicket's speaks standard German and addresses the player with "du",
capitalized only at the start of a sentence. The register is plain, warm
and spoken: a friend in the parlor talking to you, not a manual. Short
sentences, everyday words.

Key names (Enter, Escape, Space, Tab, Page Up, F1, the arrow keys Up, Down,
Left and Right, and the rest), game names and people's names stay in
English.
